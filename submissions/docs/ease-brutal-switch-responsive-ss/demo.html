Segmented Switch: Responsive Breakpoints

Resize this window (or open dev tools' device toolbar) to see the switch below adapt at 480px and 768px.

.ease-switch-brutal .ease-switch-brutal-responsive

≥ 768px: switch keeps its natural inline width.
480px–767px: segments stretch to share the full width equally.
< 480px: segments wrap onto two rows, two per row.

.ease-switch-brutal .ease-switch-vertical (unaffected reference)

.ease-switch-vertical is a fixed manual layout, not a breakpoint-driven one — it stays stacked at every viewport width. It is shown here only for comparison.

Navigate using Tab to focus and Left / Right Arrow Keys or Click to switch segments.
