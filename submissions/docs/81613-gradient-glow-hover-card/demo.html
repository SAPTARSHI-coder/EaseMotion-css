Gradient Glow Hover Card

A focused card surface where the decorative gradient appears on pointer hover or keyboard focus without affecting the content structure.

Featured experience

Product discovery

Explore a focused interface pattern with a controlled gradient glow and a persistent readable content layer.

Implementation details

The glow is decorative and is generated by a pseudo-element. The card remains a normal link, so the effect does not replace semantic interaction.

  • Hover reveals the gradient layer.
  • Keyboard focus reveals the same visual treatment.
  • Reduced-motion users are not forced to watch a transition.
  • The content remains readable when the glow is inactive.
