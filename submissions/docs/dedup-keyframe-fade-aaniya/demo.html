ease-kf-fade-in — Single Source of Truth Demo

core/animations.css and easemotion/fade.css both define an identical @keyframes ease-kf-fade-in block (opacity 0→1, scale 0.95→1). This demo reproduces that animation once, under a demo-namespaced class, to illustrate the fix without touching either restricted file.

fade-in card

Recommended fix: keep the keyframe only in core/animations.css (it's referenced by two extra selectors there: .ease-fade-in and .ease-reveal-fade), and remove the duplicate block from easemotion/fade.css so the two layers share one definition instead of drifting out of sync.
