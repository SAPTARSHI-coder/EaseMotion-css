Procedural 3D Cities

What you are looking at is not WebGL, Three.js, or an SVG image. Each city below is a single HTML <div> element. The massive 3D structures are procedurally generated during SCSS compilation using custom Trigonometric Taylor Series math to map thousands of box-shadows.
