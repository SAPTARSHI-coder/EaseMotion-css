Generative Topography

The organic contour lines you see flowing in the background are generated entirely by mathematical SCSS `repeating-radial-gradient` functions. There are zero SVG files or Canvas scripts running on this page.

Density Control

By adjusting the `$layers` and `$spacing` SCSS variables, you can effortlessly control the density and interference patterns of the contour lines.

High Interference Map

This block utilizes 10 layers of slightly offset radial gradients with a spacing of just 15px. When blended via `mix-blend-mode: screen`, it produces heavily distorted, liquid-like organic contour shapes.

Neon Terrain

By customizing the $line-color and $bg-color variables directly in the mixin, we can generate a retro-futuristic, wireframe terrain map that fits seamlessly into Cyberpunk or Synthwave aesthetics.

Micro-Topography for UI Cards

You don't just have to use this for massive backgrounds! By utilizing the @mixin generate-micro-topography helper, you can generate tiny, high-fidelity textures to apply to standard UI cards, making them feel premium and tactile.
