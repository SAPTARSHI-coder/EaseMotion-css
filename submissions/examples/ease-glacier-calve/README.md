# Glacier Calve · Ice Breaks into Water 🧊

An interactive glacier calving simulation with ice breaking into water, splashing particles, and atmospheric polar effects. Watch ice calve into the sea!

## Features

- 🧊 **Glacier Calving** – Ice breaks into water
- 🌊 **Splash Effects** – Water splashing particles
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Ice Control** – Adjust ice pieces (10-50)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset glacier
- 🔊 **Sound Effects** – Calving sounds

## How to Use

1. Click **CALVE** or press **Space** to break ice
2. Click on the canvas to calve more
3. Adjust **Speed** for animation pace
4. Adjust **Ice** for piece count
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
6. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation

## Technical Details

- **Ice Physics:** Gravity, rotation, friction
- **Splashes:** Particle system with gravity
- **Rendering:** Glowing ice with facets
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Speed:** Adjust slider range (1-10)
- **Ice:** Adjust slider range (10-50)
- **Colors:** Modify ice and water colors
- **Physics:** Adjust gravity and speed
- **Sound:** Adjust volume and frequency

## Browser Support

Works in all modern browsers:
- Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge
- Audio requires Web Audio API support

## Run Locally

Simply open `index.html` in your browser – no server required!
