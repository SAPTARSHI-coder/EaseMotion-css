ANIMATION VARIANTS

Elastic-Slide Directions

Slide Left

The notification enters from the right and settles into position with an elastic overshoot.

Slide Up

A compact bottom-positioned notification that slides upward into view.

Elastic Scale

Combines translation and scale for a subtle elastic entrance effect.

IMPLEMENTATION

Simple HTML Structure

toast.html 
<article class="toast toast--success" role="status">
    <span class="toast__icon" aria-hidden="true">✓</span>

    <div class="toast__content">
        <strong class="toast__title">
            Deployment complete
        </strong>

        <p class="toast__message">
            Your latest build is now live.
        </p>
    </div>

    <button class="toast__close"
            type="button"
            aria-label="Close notification">
        ×
    </button>
</article>
