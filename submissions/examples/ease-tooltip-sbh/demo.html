ease-tooltip

Tooltips with arrows, attribute-driven.

A hover-triggered tooltip with a small CSS-triangle arrow pointing at its trigger, fading and sliding in on hover. Positioned via a data-tooltip attribute — no extra wrapper markup for the text. Pure CSS, zero JS.

Placements

Hover (or keyboard-focus) each button.

In context

Inline text triggers

Use tooltips on inline text too — hover rate limit to see a definition, or WCAG for an acronym. They flow inline.
