Net Interest Margin Interest earned minus interest paid, over average earning assets. Settlement Window Rolling T+1 window measured from authorisation, not capture. Chargeback Ratio Disputes divided by settled volume for the same period. Reserve Coverage Liquid reserves as a percentage of the 30-day outflow forecast.
