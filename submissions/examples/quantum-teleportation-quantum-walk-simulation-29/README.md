\# Quantum Walk Simulation Component



\## Technical Overview

The Quantum Walk Simulation component is an advanced visualization module built for the EaseMotion CSS framework, simulating discrete-time quantum walks, Hadamard coin operators, conditional shift operations, and quadratic speedup dispersal patterns.

\## Quantum Walk Simulation Design

1\. \*\*Walk Flux Dynamics\*\*: The `.qwalk-flux` elements utilize multi-directional counter-rotating conic gradients to mimic superposition coin tossing and spatial position dispersion tracks.

2\. \*\*Walk Boundary Ring\*\*: The `.qwalk-boundary-ring` models lattice boundary tracking using dashed structural tracks and rotational scaling.

3\. \*\*Walk Core\*\*: The `.qwalk-core` visualizes central coin superposition nodes through rapid scale pulsations and intense emerald illumination.
