# 3D-Flip Accessible Hero Section (`#54378`)

## What does this do?
Provides an avant-garde hero landing section deck where clicking interactive toggle controls rotates the entire banner across 180-degree 3D perspective space between commercial marketing and engineering SLAs.

## How is it used?
Organize front elevator pitch and back engineering details inside an `ease-hero-3d-flip` perspective wrapper:
```html
<div class="ease-hero-3d-flip">
  <div class="hero-deck">
    <div class="panel f-panel">...</div>
    <div class="panel b-panel">...</div>
  </div>
</div>
```

## Why is it useful?
Solves issue #54378 by enabling SaaS and architectural agencies to unite high-level design vision on Side A with underlying engineering specifications on Side B via clean 3D flips.
