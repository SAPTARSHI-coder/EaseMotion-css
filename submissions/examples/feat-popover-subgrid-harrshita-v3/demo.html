Notice the perfect alignment:

The first card has a lot of text, while the second card has very little. In a standard flexbox or grid layout, the footers would be misaligned. Because these cards use grid-template-rows: subgrid, the footers of all cards in the row perfectly align to the bottom, and the headers perfectly match heights. No JS or fixed heights required!
