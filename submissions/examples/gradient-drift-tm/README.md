# Drifting gradient background

1. What does this do? A background gradient that slowly drifts through its colour stops on a long, gentle loop.

2. How is it used? Apply `drift-tm` to a section. Variants: `drift-warm-tm` (amber-ember palette), `drift-cool-tm` (midnight-blue palette). The animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

3. Why is it useful? It adds atmosphere to hero and empty-state sections without the noise of a rainbow cycle.
