# Golden Ratio · Spiral Expands in Golden Ratio 📐

An interactive golden ratio spiral that expands with mathematical precision, vibrant colors, and mesmerizing animation. Watch the spiral grow in perfect φ proportion!

## Features

- 📐 **Golden Ratio** – φ = 1.618 spiral
- 🌀 **Expand Animation** – Spiral grows outward
- 🎨 **Vibrant Colors** – Rainbow gradient
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust expansion speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Detail Control** – Adjust spiral detail (2-8)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset spiral
- ✨ **Glow Effects** – Node and line glow

## How to Use

1. Click **EXPAND** or press **Space** to grow the spiral
2. Watch the spiral expand in golden ratio
3. Adjust **Speed** for expansion pace
4. Adjust **Detail** for spiral complexity
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
6. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation
