# Neon Glow Cyberpunk Pricing Card - GSSoC 2026

A futuristic cyberpunk neon pricing card featuring continuous CSS gradient border scanning, luminous text glowing effects, and smooth hover elevation.

## 1. What does this do?
It provides a high-contrast cyberpunk pricing component with animated gradient borders, glowing typography, and energetic hover transitions.

## 2. How is it used?
Include `style.css` in your project and copy the `.neon-card` structure along with its child elements into your HTML.

## 3. Why is it useful?
It elevates gaming platforms, web3 dApps, and dark-themed tech product landing pages by offering a visually striking pricing callout.
