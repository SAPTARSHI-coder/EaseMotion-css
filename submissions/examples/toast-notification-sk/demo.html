Animation Details and Examples

Success Toast

Style: Slide From Right

Success Title

Action completed successfully.

Slide From Right starts at 120% translation offset and glides horizontally, slowing down to a halt at the edge. Highly recommended for standard notifications. 

class="toast-slide-right-in"

Error Toast

Style: Slide From Left

Error Title

Unable to complete request.

Slide From Left glides from the left edge into view. This contrasting lateral transition is great for error states, distinguishing them from standard updates. 

class="toast-slide-left-in"

Warning Toast

Style: Bounce Entry

Warning Title

Resource limit approaching.

Bounce Entry translates vertically and uses a custom cubic-bezier trajectory to overshoot and settle back down. Perfect for capturing attention immediately. 

class="toast-bounce-in"

Information Toast

Style: Fade In

Information Title

Server backup completed.

Fade In shifts opacity smoothly from 0 to 1 without translating positions. Extremely clean and ambient, designed for secondary status reports. 

class="toast-fade-in"

Loading Toast

Style: Scale In

Loading Title

Processing database sync...

Scale In utilizes 3D transforms, expanding from scale(0.85) to scale(1.0). Best combined with active progress spinner animations during backend processes. 

class="toast-scale-in"
