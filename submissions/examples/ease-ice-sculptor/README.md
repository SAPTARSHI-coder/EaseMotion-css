# 🧊 Ice Sculptor

## Sculpt Ice With Your Touch — Shape Frozen Masterpieces in Real-Time

### Description

**Ice Sculptor** is an immersive, tactile creative experience that transforms your screen into a block of crystal-clear ice. Using intuitive touch or mouse gestures, you carve, melt, and shape the ice to reveal stunning sculptures hidden within.

Watch as the ice responds to every stroke — smooth curves form under your fingers, sharp edges emerge with precise flicks, and delicate details appear with gentle taps. The ice glistens, refracts light, and even shows subtle cracks as you work, making every sculpture feel alive and authentic.

Whether you're an artist seeking a new medium or someone looking for a calming, creative escape, Ice Sculptor offers a mesmerizing way to express yourself — no chisels required.

---

### Features

- 🧊 **Realistic Ice Physics** – Ice responds naturally to pressure, speed, and duration of touch
- ✨ **Dynamic Light Refraction** – Sculptures catch and bend light as you carve, creating beautiful prismatic effects
- 👆 **Touch & Mouse Support** – Works seamlessly on both desktop and mobile devices
- 🎨 **Infinite Possibilities** – Each session starts with a fresh block of ice — create anything from abstract forms to recognizable shapes
- ❄️ **Melting & Preservation** – Watch your sculpture slowly melt or freeze it in time to admire your work
- 🌈 **Color & Texture Variations** – Experiment with different ice types (clear, cloudy, colored, or sparkling)
- 💾 **Save Your Art** – Export your masterpiece as a PNG image

---

### How to Use

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required
2. **Touch or click** on the ice block to begin sculpting
3. **Drag** to carve away layers and shape the ice
4. **Hold** to melt and smooth surfaces
5. **Select tools** from the toolbar:
   - ⛏️ **Carve** – Remove ice with broad strokes
   - 🔥 **Melt** – Soften and blur edges
   - ✨ **Smooth** – Flatten and polish surfaces
   - 🔪 **Chisel** – Make sharp, precise cuts
6. **Customize** the ice color and sparkle effect
7. **Save** your sculpture as a PNG image
8. **Reset** to start fresh with a new block of ice
