# Snail Trail · Leaves Sparkly Trail Behind 🐌

An interactive snail simulation that leaves a sparkling, shimmering trail behind as it crawls. Click to guide the snail and watch the magical sparkly trail appear!

## Features

- 🐌 **Realistic Snail** – Detailed shell, body, head, eyes, and antennae
- ✨ **Sparkly Trail** – Glowing, shimmering particles follow behind
- 🎨 **Animated Shell** – Shell with spiral pattern and highlight
- 📡 **Animated Antennae** – Antennae sway with lifelike movement
- 🎮 **Click to Guide** – Click anywhere to direct the snail
- 🌟 **Trail Particles** – Sparkle, glow, and star-shaped particles
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation with button
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset snail to starting position
- 🌿 **Garden Environment** – Forest floor with light rays

## How to Use

1. Watch the snail crawl and leave a sparkly trail
2. **Click** anywhere on the canvas to guide the snail
3. See the sparkling trail follow behind with glow and shimmer
4. Press **PAUSE** to pause/resume animation
5. Adjust **Speed** slider to control animation pace
6. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset

## Controls

| Action | Method |
|--------|--------|
| Guide Snail | Click canvas |
| Pause/Resume | Click button |

## Trail Particle Effects

- **Glow**: Soft radial gradient around each particle
- **Sparkle**: Bright core with shimmer
- **Stars**: Some particles form star shapes
- **Colors**: Gold/shimmer hue range (40-70°)
- **Lifespan**: Particles fade out over time

## Technical Details

- **Snail Movement**: Smooth wandering with click-based guidance
- **Trail System**: Particle-based trail with glow and sparkle effects
- **Particle Physics**: Life decay, subtle wobble, and color variation
- **Animation**: Body wave, antennae sway, shell spiral
- **Performance**: 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Snail Size:** Modify `this.size = 22`
- **Speed:** Adjust `this.speed = 0.4`
- **Trail Interval:** Change `this.trailInterval = 3`
- **Trail Particle Count:** Modify loop in trail creation
- **Colors:** Adjust hue, sat, and light values in TrailParticle
