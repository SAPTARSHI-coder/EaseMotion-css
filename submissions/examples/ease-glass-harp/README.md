# 🍷 Glass Harp

## Glasses Vibrate With Different Tones — Musical Glass Instrument

### Description

**Glass Harp** is a mesmerizing, interactive musical experience that brings the ethereal beauty of a glass harp to your screen. Each glass vibrates with its own unique tone when touched, creating a symphony of shimmering light and sound. Like running your finger around the rim of a crystal glass, the glasses come alive with pure, resonant tones that ripple through the air.

The experience is magical and deeply satisfying. Seven beautifully rendered crystal glasses sit in a row, each filled with different levels of water to create distinct musical notes — from the deep, resonant C5 to the bright, shimmering B5. When you click a glass, it comes alive with vibration — the glass shimmers, ripples of energy radiate outward, and particles of light dance around it like musical notes made visible. Each glass glows with its own unique color, creating a visual symphony that matches the auditory experience.

The visual richness is extraordinary. Each glass is meticulously rendered with transparent crystal, subtle reflections, and shimmering highlights. The water inside ripples and dances with each vibration, creating concentric waves that spread across the surface. Colored glows radiate from each glass when played, and sparkling particles burst outward like musical notes given form. The glasses vibrate with realistic physics — they wobble, shimmer, and gradually return to stillness, just like real crystal glasses.

But this is more than just a beautiful animation — it's a fully interactive musical instrument. Click any glass to play its tone, or press the corresponding number key (1-7). Play all the glasses at once for a cascading arpeggio, or enable auto-play for a mesmerizing continuous performance. Adjust the **vibration intensity** to control how much the glasses shimmer and glow. Control the **sustain** to make the vibrations last longer or fade quickly. Fine-tune the **pitch** to shift the entire scale up or down for different musical moods.

Whether you're a musician exploring new sounds, a meditator seeking a calming visual focus, or simply someone who appreciates the beauty of crystal and light, Glass Harp delivers a stunning, responsive experience that transforms touch into music and vibration into art.

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Click any glass** to hear its unique tone and watch it vibrate with light

3. **Press number keys 1-7** to play individual glasses from your keyboard

4. **Press Space** to play all glasses in a cascading arpeggio

5. **Adjust the vibration** slider to control shimmer intensity (10-100%)

6. **Adjust the sustain** slider to control how long notes resonate (10-100%)

7. **Adjust the pitch** slider to shift the scale up or down (-50 to +50)

8. **Enable auto-play** with the ▶️ Auto button for a mesmerizing continuous performance

9. **Reset** with the 🔄 Reset button to clear all vibrations
