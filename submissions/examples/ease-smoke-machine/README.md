# Smoke Machine · Smoke Billows on Command 💨

An interactive smoke machine that billows atmospheric smoke on command with particle effects, realistic physics, and ambient sound.

## Features

- 💨 **Smoke Machine** – Billows atmospheric smoke
- 🎚️ **Density Control** – Adjust smoke density (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Wind Control** – Adjust wind direction (1-10)
- 🔊 **Sound Effects** – Low rumble and hiss
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🗑️ **Clear** – Clear all smoke
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset everything
- 📊 **Counter** – Tracks particles and billows

## How to Use

1. Click **BILLOW** or press **Space** to release smoke
2. Click on the canvas to billow more
3. Adjust **Density** for thicker/thinner smoke
4. Adjust **Wind** for direction
5. Click **CLEAR** or press **C** to clear all
6. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
7. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation

## Technical Details

- **Particle System:** 15-65 particles per billow
- **Physics:** Gravity, turbulence, growth
- **Rendering:** Soft radial gradients
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Density:** Adjust slider range (1-10)
- **Wind:** Adjust slider range (1-10)
- **Physics:** Adjust gravity, turbulence, growth
- **Colors:** Modify smoke color
- **Sound:** Adjust volume and frequency
