Magnetic Button

Move your pointer around the button and watch it subtly follow your movement.

01

Responsive

The button follows the pointer according to its distance from the center of the interactive area.

02

Restrained

A configurable movement limit keeps the interaction subtle and prevents the control from moving too far away.

03

Lightweight

The effect uses pointer events, CSS transforms, and requestAnimationFrame without external dependencies.
