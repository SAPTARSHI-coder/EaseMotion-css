# Mandala Spin · Mandala Spins and Pulses 🪷

An interactive mandala with spinning and pulsing animation, intricate geometric patterns, and vibrant colors. Watch the mandala rotate and breathe!

## Features

- 🪷 **Mandala** – Intricate geometric pattern
- 🌀 **Spin Animation** – Smooth rotation
- 💫 **Pulse Effect** – Gentle breathing motion
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust spin speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Layer Control** – Adjust layer count (3-8)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- ⏹️ **Stop** – Instant stop
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset rotation

## How to Use

1. Click **SPIN** or press **Space** to start the mandala
2. Click **STOP** or press **Space** again to stop
3. Adjust **Speed** for rotation pace
4. Adjust **Layers** for pattern complexity
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
6. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation
