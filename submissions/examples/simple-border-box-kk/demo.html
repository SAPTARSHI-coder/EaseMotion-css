Simple Border Box

Subtle bordered containers for grouped notes, settings, and content.

This demo shows a lightweight CSS-only border box utility for wrapping small content blocks, settings groups, notes, or section content. It is intentionally simple and reusable across many layouts.

Account Note

Use a basic border box to separate small pieces of supportive content from the surrounding layout.

Settings Group

This variation keeps the same reusable structure while adding a slightly softer background surface.

Information Block

The utility works well for compact notes, grouped settings, and lightweight content wrappers.

HTML Usage

<div class="simple-border-box">
  <p>Content goes here.</p>
</div>
