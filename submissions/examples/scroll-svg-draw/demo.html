How it works

  • pathLength="1" normalises the SVG path so stroke-dasharray: 1 always equals the full length.
  • stroke-dashoffset: 1 → 0 inside @keyframes progressively reveals the stroke.
  • animation-timeline: view() ties the keyframe progress to the element's scroll position in the viewport.
  • animation-range: entry 0% entry 80% begins drawing on viewport entry and finishes before exit.
  • The entire animation is wrapped in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) so motion-sensitive users are unaffected.
