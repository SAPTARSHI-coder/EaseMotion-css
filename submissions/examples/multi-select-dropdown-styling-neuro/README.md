
---

## 🚀 Usage
1. Clone or copy the files into your project.
2. Open `demo.html` in your browser.
3. Hover over the dropdown to reveal multi-select options.

---

## 🛠️ Customization
- **Colors** → Adjust background and accent colors in `.dropdown-toggle` and `input[type="checkbox"]`.
- **Animation speed** → Modify `transition: 0.6s ease`.
- **Menu items** → Add/remove `<li>` elements with checkboxes.

---

## 📖 Accessibility
- Uses `role="listbox"` and `aria-label` for screen readers.
- Checkboxes are keyboard accessible.
