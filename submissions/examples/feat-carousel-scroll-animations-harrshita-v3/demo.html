ease-carousel Scroll-Driven Animations

Zero-JavaScript Scroll Effects:

1. Notice the progress bar at the very top of the dark container below. It grows as you scroll down using animation-timeline: scroll().

2. Scroll down to see cards reveal themselves gracefully using animation-timeline: view(). No Intersection Observers needed!

Note: Scroll-driven animations are a cutting-edge CSS feature. They work in modern Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge). Fallbacks should be provided for Safari/Firefox.

View-Driven Reveal 1

I animated into view based on my position in the scrollport, not based on time! This uses animation-timeline: view().

As I enter the viewport, my opacity and transform interpolate automatically based on the scroll position. If you scroll back up, the animation reverses natively!
