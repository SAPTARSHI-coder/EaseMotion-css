3D Origami Accordion Cards

Paper-fold accordion component featuring multidirectional 3D perspective transforms, shadow elevation shading, and zero JS panel expansion.

Standardizes tokenized custom property mappings (`--accent-color`, `--radius-full`, `--ease-elastic`) across cross-platform UI component libraries.

Isolates keyframe transitions on dedicated GPU compositor layers (`will-change: transform`), preventing layout thrashing and frame drops.

Employs organic spring cubic bezier curves (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`) inspired by fluid physical motion dynamics.
