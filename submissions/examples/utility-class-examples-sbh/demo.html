Utility class examples & live demos.

Practical, visual demonstrations of common animation utility classes — Fade In, Slide Up, Zoom In, Bounce — plus Delay and Duration utilities. Each card shows the utility name, a live preview box, and the class name to use. Press Replay to re-run every animation from the start.
