Aurora Glow Accordion

An atmospheric accordion inspired by northern lights, built without JavaScript.

01 What is Aurora Glow?

Aurora Glow is a CSS-only accordion that combines soft atmospheric gradients, glass surfaces and animated light effects to create a modern interface.

02 Does it require JavaScript?

No. Native HTML <details> and <summary> elements provide the interaction without JavaScript.

03 Is it accessible?

Yes. The native disclosure behavior provides keyboard interaction and works naturally with assistive technologies.

04 Is the component responsive?

Yes. The layout scales smoothly across desktop, tablet and mobile screens while maintaining comfortable spacing.
