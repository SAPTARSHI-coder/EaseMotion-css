# 🕯️ Candle Glow

## Warm Candle Glow With Flicker — Mesmerizing Ambient Light

### Description

**Candle Glow** is a stunning, atmospheric simulation that brings the warm, comforting light of a candle to life on your screen. Watch as a beautifully rendered candle flickers and glows, casting a soft, golden light that transforms the surrounding darkness into a cozy, intimate space. The flame dances with organic, unpredictable movement, while tiny sparks drift upward like fireflies in the warm air.

The experience is deeply soothing and meditative. The candle sits centered in a dark void, its flame flickering with gentle, hypnotic rhythms. A warm, amber glow radiates outward, illuminating the candle's wax body, casting subtle shadows, and creating a sense of depth and presence. The flame is not a simple static shape — it's a complex, dynamic form that pulses, sways, and dances with life. Tiny particles of light break away from the flame, rising and fading like embers carried by an invisible breeze.

But this is more than just a visual — it's a fully interactive experience. Control the **intensity** of the flame from a subtle ember to a blazing fire. Adjust the **flicker** amount to make the flame dance wildly or burn steady and calm. Fine-tune the **warmth** to shift the color from cool white to deep amber. Pause the animation to admire the frozen moment, reset to start fresh, or blow out the candle with a click and watch as smoke gracefully rises from the extinguished wick.

Whether you're seeking a calming screensaver for relaxation, a cozy ambiance for your workspace, a beautiful visual for meditation, or simply a moment of peace in a busy day, Candle Glow delivers with stunning visual quality, mesmerizing animations, and intuitive controls that let you shape the mood.

---

### Features

- 🕯️ **Realistic Candle Rendering** — Detailed wax body, glowing wick, and dynamic flame with organic movement

- 🔥 **Dynamic Flame Physics** — The flame responds to intensity, flicker, and warmth settings with realistic, unpredictable movement

- 🌟 **Particle System** — Tiny embers and sparks rise from the flame, creating a magical, atmospheric effect

- 💨 **Blow Out Effect** — Click to blow out the candle and watch smoke gracefully rise, or relight with another click

- 🎚️ **Three Adjustable Parameters** — Intensity (10-100%), Flicker (0-100%), Warmth (0-100%)

- ⏸️ **Pause & Resume** — Freeze the moment to admire the candle's beauty

- 🎨 **Warm Golden Palette** — Rich amber, gold, and orange tones that evoke comfort and coziness

- 📊 **Real-Time Status** — Track candle status and flicker intensity at a glance

- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Control** — Space to pause, R to reset, B to blow/relight, Arrow keys for intensity

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Support** — Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

---

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Watch the candle glow** as the flame flickers and dances

3. **Adjust the intensity** slider to control flame brightness and size (10-100%)

4. **Adjust the flicker** slider to control how much the flame dances (0-100%)

5. **Adjust the warmth** slider to shift the color from warm amber to cool gold

6. **Pause the animation** with the ⏸️ button or press Space

7. **Blow out the candle** with the 💨 button or press B — watch the smoke rise

8. **Relight the candle** by clicking the same button again

9. **Reset** with the 🔄 button or press R
