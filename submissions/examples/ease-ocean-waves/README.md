# 🌊 Ocean Waves

## Crashing Waves Sound and Visual — Immersive Ocean Experience

### Description

**Ocean Waves** is a breathtaking, immersive simulation that brings the powerful, soothing sound and mesmerizing visual of crashing ocean waves to your screen. Like standing on a peaceful shore, the waves roll in endlessly, each one a moment of beauty and calm, creating a meditative soundscape that transports you to the edge of the sea.

The experience is immersive, powerful, and deeply calming. A vast ocean stretches before you under a beautiful sky, with waves rolling in from the horizon. Each wave builds and swells as it approaches, cresting with a brilliant white foam before crashing onto the shore with a powerful, satisfying burst. The waves are not just flat animations — they're dynamic, three-dimensional forms with depth, highlights, and realistic motion. When a wave crashes, it explodes into a shower of sparkling spray and foam particles that dance and settle like sea foam on the sand.

The visual richness is extraordinary and endlessly captivating. The ocean surface shimmers with subtle highlights, catching the light of a distant sun or moon. Waves roll in with organic, unpredictable timing — some gentle and small, others powerful and dramatic. Each wave is beautifully rendered with gradient colors, foaming crests, and realistic physics. When they crash, the explosion of white foam and sparkling spray creates a moment of pure visual poetry, followed by the gentle settling of foam particles as the wave retreats back into the sea.

The audio experience is equally rich and authentic. Using the Web Audio API, the ocean soundscape is synthesized in real-time — a continuous, gentle roar of the sea punctuated by powerful crash sounds. The noise of the ocean is layered and complex, with the deep rumble of distant waves and the sharp, explosive sound of waves breaking on the shore. The audio responds to the intensity setting, creating a truly immersive experience that feels like standing by the ocean.

But this is more than just a beautiful animation — it's an interactive experience that puts you in control of the sea. Click the canvas or press Space to trigger a powerful **crash** wave at any moment. Adjust the **intensity** to control the power and frequency of the waves — from a gentle, lapping sea to a powerful, stormy surf. Control the **speed** to change how fast the waves roll in. Adjust the **foam** amount for more or less visual foam and spray. Toggle the sound on and off, or reset to start fresh.

Whether you're seeking a calming, meditative experience to relax your mind, a beautiful interactive art piece for contemplation, a soothing soundscape for sleep or deep focus, a gentle background for creative work, or simply a moment of connection with the ocean, Ocean Waves delivers a stunning, responsive experience that captures the peaceful, powerful magic of the sea.

### Features

- 🌊 **Realistic Wave Physics** — Dynamic waves with organic motion, foam crests, and crashing effects

- 🔊 **Authentic Ocean Sound** — Synthesized ocean noise with wave crash sounds using Web Audio API

- 💧 **Foam and Spray Particles** — Beautiful white foam and sparkling spray on wave crashes

- 🎚️ **Adjustable Intensity** — Control wave power and frequency from gentle lapping to powerful surf (10-100%)

- 🎵 **Wave Speed Control** — Adjust how fast waves roll in (10-100%)

- 💧 **Foam Amount Control** — Control visual foam and spray density (0-100%)

- 🌊 **Crash Wave Button** — Trigger a powerful wave crash anytime

- 🌅 **Beautiful Atmosphere** — Dynamic sky with sun/moon and ocean shimmer

- 📊 **Real-Time Status** — Track wave count, intensity, and ocean state

- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Control** — Space to crash, T to toggle, R to reset, arrow keys for intensity

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Support** — Click or tap to crash waves on any device

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Listen to the ocean** — waves roll in with authentic sound

3. **Watch the waves** — dynamic waves with foam and spray

4. **Click the canvas** (or press Space) to trigger a powerful crash wave

5. **Adjust the intensity** slider for gentle lapping or powerful surf (10-100%)

6. **Adjust the speed** slider for faster or slower waves (10-100%)

7. **Adjust the foam** slider for more or less visual foam (0-100%)

8. **Toggle the sound** on/off with the 🔊 Toggle button or press T

9. **Reset** with the 🔄 Reset button or press R
