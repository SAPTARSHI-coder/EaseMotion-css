# Spotlight Circle · Spotlight Follows Cursor 🔦

An interactive spotlight circle that follows your cursor, revealing hidden content with dramatic lighting effects and glowing visuals.

## Features

- 🔦 **Spotlight Circle** – Moves with your cursor
- 🎯 **Reveal Effect** – Hidden elements appear in light
- 🎚️ **Size Control** – Adjust spotlight size (30-150px)
- 🎚️ **Intensity Control** – Adjust light intensity (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset spotlight position
- ✨ **Glow Effects** – Dramatic lighting

## How to Use

1. **Move your mouse** or **drag your finger** on the canvas
2. Watch the spotlight follow your cursor
3. Hidden shapes and colors are revealed
4. Adjust **Size** for spotlight radius
5. Adjust **Intensity** for brightness
6. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset position
7. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation

## Technical Details

- **Spotlight:** Circular clip with gradient glow
- **Reveal:** Hidden elements with brightness
- **Controls:** Real-time parameter adjustment
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:

- **Intensity:** Adjust slider range (1-10)
- **Colors:** Modify shape colors
- **Elements:** Add/remove hidden elements
- **Glow:** Adjust glow intensity
