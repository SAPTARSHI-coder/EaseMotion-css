\# Plasma Energy Shield Component



A futuristic plasma defense component featuring expanding energy shockwave ripples, a radiant thermal core, and glassmorphism interface controls built for EaseMotion CSS.



\## Location

\- Directory: `submissions/examples/plasma-energy-shield-kl/`



\## Files Included

\- `demo.html`: Interactive preview window showcasing the plasma energy shield simulation.

\- `style.css`: Scoped plasma styling and keyframe rules (`ease-plasma-shockwave`, `ease-plasma-pulse`).

\- `README.md`: Documentation for the component submission.
