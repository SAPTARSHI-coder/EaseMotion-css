# 💓 Heartbeat Monitor

## Heartbeat Line Pulses — Medical Monitor Simulation

### Description

**Heartbeat Monitor** is a mesmerizing, cinematic simulation of a medical heart monitor that brings the rhythmic pulse of a beating heart to life on your screen. Watch as the iconic heartbeat line pulses across the display, each beat marked by a bright flash and a rhythmic wave that captures the essence of life itself.

The experience is both calming and captivating. A dark, medical-grade monitor screen fills your view, complete with subtle grid lines and professional interface elements. Across the screen, a glowing green line traces the rhythm of a heartbeat — the sharp spike of the QRS complex followed by the gentle curve of the T-wave, just like a real electrocardiogram (ECG). With each beat, the line pulses brighter, a heart icon glows, and the monitor comes alive with the rhythm of life.

The visual quality is authentic and polished. The heartbeat waveform is scientifically accurate — featuring the P-wave (atrial contraction), the tall QRS complex (ventricular contraction), and the T-wave (ventricular repolarization). The line glows with a medical green color, pulsing brighter with each heartbeat. The monitor includes professional elements like grid lines, corner accents, and status indicators. As the heart beats, a bright spot travels along the waveform, leaving a soft glow trail behind it.

But this is more than just a beautiful animation — it's an interactive medical monitor you control. Adjust the **BPM** to change the heart rate from a slow 30 BPM to a rapid 180 BPM, just like a real patient monitor. Control the **amplitude** to make the heartbeat waves taller or shorter. Adjust the **glow** intensity for the perfect visual feel. Switch between **six monitoring modes** — Normal, Fast, Slow, Irregular, Stress, and Calm — each with its own unique rhythm and color signature. Pause the beat to examine the waveform, reset to start fresh, or simply watch the rhythm of life unfold.

Whether you're a medical professional studying cardiac rhythms, a student learning about ECG waveforms, a creator seeking atmospheric visuals, or simply someone fascinated by the rhythm of life, Heartbeat Monitor delivers a stunning, authentic experience that captures the essence of a beating heart.

### ECG Waveform Guide

| Wave | Description | What It Represents |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **P-wave** | Small upward bump | Atrial contraction (atria pumping) |
| **QRS Complex** | Tall sharp spike | Ventricular contraction (main heartbeat) |
| **T-wave** | Gentle upward curve | Ventricular repolarization (recovery) |
