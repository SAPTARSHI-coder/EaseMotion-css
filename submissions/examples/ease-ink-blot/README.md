# Ink Blot · Rorschach Ink Blot Forms 🖤

An interactive Rorschach ink blot generator that forms organic, symmetrical patterns with fluid ink spreading and subtle animation. Click to generate unique ink blot patterns!

## Features

- 🖤 **Organic Ink Blots** – Fluid, organic ink blot formations
- 🎨 **Symmetrical Patterns** – Classic Rorschach symmetry
- 🌊 **Fluid Movement** – Ink droplets drift and pulse
- 🎲 **Random Generation** – Each blot is unique
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation with Space or button
- 🔄 **Reset** – Clear canvas
- 📋 **Keyboard Shortcuts** – N for new blot, Space to pause, R to reset

## How to Use

1. Click **NEW BLOT** or press **N** to generate a new ink blot
2. Watch the ink form organically with fluid motion
3. Each blot is unique with different shapes and patterns
4. Press **Space** or click **PAUSE** to pause/resume animation
5. Adjust **Speed** slider to control animation pace
6. Press **R** or click **RESET** to clear the canvas

## Technical Details

- **Droplet System:** 60-80 droplets with organic movement
- **Symmetry:** Horizontal mirroring for Rorschach effect
- **Organic Shapes:** Distorted polygons with gradient fills
- **Animation:** Gentle drifting, pulsing, and growth
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Droplet Count:** Modify `numDroplets` range (40-80)
- **Droplet Size:** Adjust `size` range (5-30px)
- **Movement Speed:** Modify `vx` and `vy` values
- **Growth Amount:** Change `growAmount` range (0.3-0.5)
- **Distortion:** Adjust `distortion` values (0.2-0.4)
