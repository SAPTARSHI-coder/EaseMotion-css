# 🕯️ Mood Candle

## Flame Color Changes With Interaction — Emotional Light Experience

### Description

**Mood Candle** is an interactive, emotionally responsive candle that changes its flame color based on your touch, click, or drag. Like a magical candle that reads your mood, the flame transforms through a spectrum of colors — from warm golden amber to cool serene blue, from passionate red to peaceful green — responding to every interaction with a beautiful, glowing transition.

The experience is intimate and responsive. A beautifully detailed candle sits in a dark, atmospheric space, its flame flickering with warm, organic movement. As you touch, click, or drag across the candle, the flame color shifts smoothly through the rainbow, creating a direct, emotional connection between your action and the visual response. Drag horizontally to cycle through hues, drag vertically to shift brightness, or let the candle cycle through pre-set moods.

The visual richness is extraordinary. The flame is rendered with stunning detail — a glowing core, flickering edges, and soft, atmospheric light that bathes the surrounding space in the flame's color. The candle itself is meticulously crafted with wax drips, a glowing wick, and a warm melt pool. Tiny particles of light dance up from the flame like fireflies, each one carrying the flame's color. When you interact, a beautiful color ring pulses around the flame, and the glow intensifies, creating a deeply satisfying feedback loop.

But this is more than just a pretty light — it's an emotional tool. Choose from pre-set **mood modes** — Calm, Warm, Cool, Romantic, Nature, Sunset, Ocean, Aurora, and Rainbow — each with its own emotional resonance. Or take control with the hue slider and create your own perfect color. Adjust the brightness to match your mood, and control the flicker for a steady meditative flame or a lively dancing fire. Toggle the candle on and off, or reset to its warm default glow.

Whether you're seeking a calming visual for meditation, an ambient light for your workspace, an interactive art piece, or simply a beautiful way to explore color and emotion, Mood Candle delivers a deeply satisfying, responsive experience that feels alive.

### Features

- 🕯️ **Interactive Color Control** — Click, drag, or touch the canvas to change flame color instantly

- 🎨 **Full Spectrum Colors** — Smooth transitions through the entire rainbow (0-360° hue)

- 🌀 **Eight Mood Modes** — Calm, Warm, Cool, Romantic, Nature, Sunset, Ocean, Aurora, Rainbow

- 💡 **Adjustable Brightness** — Control flame intensity from subtle glow to brilliant blaze (20-100%)

- 🌊 **Flicker Control** — Adjust flame movement from steady meditation to lively dance (0-100%)

- 🔥 **Toggle On/Off** — Turn the candle on or off with a click

- 🎯 **Real-Time Feedback** — Instant visual response with color ring, intensity boost, and glow

- 📊 **Live Color Display** — See the current hex code and hue value

- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Control** — Space to toggle, R to reset, M for mode, arrow keys for hue

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Support** — Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile

---

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Click or drag on the candle** to change the flame color — the flame responds instantly

3. **Cycle through moods** with the 🌀 Mode button or press M

4. **Adjust the hue** slider for precise color control (0-360°)

5. **Adjust the brightness** slider for intensity (20-100%)

6. **Adjust the flicker** slider for flame movement (0-100%)

7. **Toggle the candle** on/off with the 🔥 Toggle button or press Space

8. **Reset to warm default** with the 🎨 Reset button or press R
