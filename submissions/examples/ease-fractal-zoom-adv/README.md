# Fractal Zoom · Zoom into Infinite Fractal 🌀

An interactive fractal zoom into the infinite Mandelbrot set with smooth zooming, vibrant colors, and deep exploration capabilities.

## Features

- 🌀 **Mandelbrot Fractal** – Infinite mathematical beauty
- 🔍 **Zoom In/Out** – Smooth zoom animation
- 🎨 **Vibrant Colors** – Smooth HSL coloring
- 🎚️ **Depth Control** – Adjust iteration depth (50-500)
- 🎚️ **Zoom Speed Control** – Adjust zoom speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset to initial view
- 🖱️ **Click to Zoom** – Click on canvas to zoom in

## How to Use

1. Click **ZOOM IN** or press **+** to zoom in
2. Click **ZOOM OUT** or press **-** to zoom out
3. Click directly on the canvas to zoom into that area
4. Adjust **Depth** for detail level
5. Adjust **Zoom Speed** for animation pace
6. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
7. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation
