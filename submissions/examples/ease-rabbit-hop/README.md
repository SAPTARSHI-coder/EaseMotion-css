# Rabbit Hop · Hops Away on Interaction 🐰

An interactive rabbit that hops away when approached, featuring realistic hopping animation, twitching nose, fluffy tail, and ear wiggles. Move your mouse near the rabbit to watch it hop away!

## Features

- 🐰 **Realistic Rabbit** – Detailed body, head, ears, eyes, nose, and fluffy tail
- 🐇 **Hop Animation** – Smooth hopping with arc trajectory and squish effect
- 👃 **Twitching Nose** – Animated nose with subtle twitch
- 👂 **Ear Wiggles** – Ears wiggle with lifelike motion
- 🐾 **Fluffy Tail** – Detailed tail with fluff particles
- 🎮 **Proximity Interaction** – Rabbit hops away when mouse/touch approaches
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation with button
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset rabbit to starting position
- 🌿 **Meadow Environment** – Grass, flowers, and light rays

## How to Use

1. Move your mouse or drag your finger near the rabbit
2. Watch the rabbit hop away with a realistic arc
3. The rabbit will wander when not being chased
4. Press **PAUSE** to pause/resume animation
