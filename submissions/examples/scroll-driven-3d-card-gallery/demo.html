Scroll-Driven 3D Card Gallery

Cards rotate, unfurl, and emerge from 3D space as the user scrolls — using animation-timeline: view() with CSS perspective transforms. Each card gets its own independent scroll timeline. Zero JavaScript.

🔄

Y-Axis Flip

The card rotates in from the left on its Y-axis using perspective(1000px) rotateY(-25deg), then snaps flush as the scroll position reaches the optimal viewing angle. Perfect for horizontal slide-in reveals.

⚡

X-Axis Unfurl

Cards pivot downward from a tilted angle using rotateX(-30deg) and translateY(40px) for a dramatic top-down reveal effect — like cards unfurling from a stack.

📐

Diagonal 3D Slide

Combines rotateX and rotateY with translateY and translateX for a compound 3D motion. Cards slide in diagonally with realistic depth and perspective.

🔍

Z-Axis Depth Zoom

Cards zoom in from deep Z-space using translateZ(-200px) combined with scale(0.8) and a blur filter — creating cinematic depth-of-field effects as the card comes into focus.
