Modern CSS Technique

Scroll-Driven
Depth Morph

Cards that lift off the page as you scroll — progressive box-shadow, scale, blur, and background-shift all driven by @property + animation-timeline. The illusion of depth responds to scroll position. Zero JavaScript.

Scroll for depth
Technique 1

Shadow
That Deepens

Cards with box-shadow that intensifies from subtle to dramatic as you scroll. The shadow's blur, spread, and opacity are all controlled by scroll-driven custom properties — creating the illusion of cards lifting closer to the viewer.

◆

Shadow Lift

The card appears to rise toward you as the shadow grows deeper and wider.

◆

Progressive Depth

Every pixel of scroll contributes to a stronger, more dramatic shadow.

◆

Multi-layer Shadow

Multiple shadow layers create a realistic depth illusion that builds over time.

Technique 2

Scale
That Approaches

Cards that scale up as the section enters the viewport, creating the illusion of floating toward the reader. @property --depth-scale with <number> syntax enables smooth interpolation from 0.9 to 1.05.

◆

Scale Up

The card grows slightly larger and more prominent as you scroll down.

◆

Subtle Zoom

A gentle scale increase draws attention without being distracting.

◆

Focus Effect

Combined with shadow, this creates a pronounced lift-off effect.

Technique 3

Blur
That Clears

Cards that start slightly blurred and gradually sharpen as you scroll into them. @property --depth-blur drives the CSS filter: blur() from 4px down to 0, creating a focus-pull effect that guides reader attention.

◆

Focus Pull

Cards snap into sharp focus as the section enters the viewport center.

◆

Attention Guide

Blur naturally guides the eye — sharp content demands attention.

◆

Crisp Detail

All text and borders become razor-sharp by the time the section centers.

CSS APIs Used

The modern CSS primitives powering scroll-driven visual depth
API Role Support
@property Registers typed custom properties — enables smooth interpolation of shadow, scale, and blur values Chrome 85+, Safari 16.4+, Firefox 128+
animation-timeline: view() Links visual depth animation to element visibility within the scroll container Chrome 115+, Safari 18+
box-shadow Creates the illusion of depth through multi-layered shadows All modern browsers
filter: blur() Creates focus-pull effect — cards sharpen as they approach viewport center Chrome 53+, Safari 9.1+, Firefox 35+
transform: scale() Creates approach effect — cards appear to float toward the reader All modern browsers

In browsers without @property or animation-timeline, static fallback values provide fully styled cards at a reasonable depth level.
