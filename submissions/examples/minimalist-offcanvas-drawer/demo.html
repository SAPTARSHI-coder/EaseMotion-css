M.

Less is more.

Note: Built with 100% pure CSS (Zero JS). Utilizes the Checkbox Hack pattern to manage open/close states globally.

This variant embodies the Minimalist aesthetic:

  • Typography: Relies on elegant, sparse typography and generous whitespace.
  • Motion: Utilizes a very long, elegant easing curve (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)) for a sophisticated feel.
  • Absence of Clutter: No heavy drop shadows. Just a single crisp border and a light frosted glass backdrop.
