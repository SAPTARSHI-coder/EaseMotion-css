ease-scroll-reveal

Scroll down — each card fades and rises into view as it enters the viewport. Pure CSS via scroll-driven animations.

01 · Origins

Every motion system starts with a single frame. This card reveals as it crosses the viewport edge.

02 · Momentum

Once in motion, easing carries the eye. This card slides in from the left as it enters view.

03 · Settling

A good reveal ends calm. This card slides in from the right and settles into place.

04 · Depth

Scale adds dimension. This card grows into view from a smaller, faded state.

05 · Focus

Blur reveals read like a lens focusing. This card sharpens as it enters the viewport.

06 · Closure

Reveals should never compete with content. Each one is subtle, short, and stops when the card is centered.
