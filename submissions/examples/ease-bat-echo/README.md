# Bat Echo · Sonar Waves from Bat 🦇

An interactive bat simulation featuring sonar waves, echo location pulses, and flapping wings. Move your mouse near the bat to trigger sonar pulses and watch the bat respond!

## Features

- 🦇 **Detailed Bat** – Body, wings, ears, eyes, and feet
- 📡 **Sonar Waves** – Expanding ring pulses with glow effects
- 🎵 **Echo Location** – Sonar pulses triggered by mouse proximity
- 🪶 **Wing Flaps** – Animated wings with realistic motion
- 🎮 **Proximity Interaction** – Bat responds when mouse approaches
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation with button
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset bat to starting position
- 🌙 **Night Environment** – Moon, stars, and cave silhouettes

## How to Use

1. Move your mouse or drag your finger near the bat
2. Watch sonar waves pulse from the bat
3. The bat will move toward your cursor with curiosity
4. Sonar rings expand with glow and directional pulses
5. Press **PAUSE** to pause/resume animation
6. Adjust **Speed** slider to control animation pace
7. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset

## Controls

| Action | Method |
|--------|--------|
| Trigger Sonar | Move mouse near bat / Touch near bat |
| Pause/Resume | Click button |
| Reset | Click reset / R key |
| Speed | Drag slider (1-10) |
