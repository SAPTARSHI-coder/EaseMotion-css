# Klein Bottle · Bottle Twists Through Itself 🧪

An interactive 3D Klein bottle that twists through itself with smooth transformations, vibrant colors, and glowing effects.

## Features

- 🧪 **3D Klein Bottle** – Mathematical non-orientable surface
- 🌀 **Twist Animation** – Self-intersecting morph
- 🎨 **Vibrant Colors** – Rainbow gradient
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust twist speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Detail Control** – Adjust mesh detail (2-6)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset bottle
- ✨ **Glow Effects** – Vertex glow and edge glow

## How to Use

1. Click **TWIST** or press **Space** to twist the bottle
2. Watch it deform and self-intersect
3. Adjust **Speed** for twist pace
4. Adjust **Detail** for mesh complexity
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
6. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation

## Controls

## Technical Details

- **3D Rendering:** Perspective projection
- **Mesh:** Triangle-based surface rendering
- **Morphing:** Smooth interpolation
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Colors:** Modify color generation
- **Detail:** Adjust slider range (2-6)
- **Speed:** Modify twist speed
- **Mesh:** Change U/V segments
- **Klein:** Modify parametric equations
