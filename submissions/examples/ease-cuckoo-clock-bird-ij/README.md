# ease-cuckoo-clock-bird
A carved Black Forest cuckoo clock with a peaked roof, a cream clock face, a bird that pops from its door on the hour, and a swinging pinecone pendulum.
## Run
Open `demo.html` directly in a browser to see the bird pop out.
## Notes
- The bird slides out and back inside the door every twelve seconds.
- The pendulum swings side to side while the chime lamp blinks gold.
