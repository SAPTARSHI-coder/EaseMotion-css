How the reveal works

1

An oversized ::before

A pseudo-element roughly 2.2× the parent's size sits behind the content, anchored to one corner so it has room to sweep across the whole element when scaled up.

2

Scaled to zero by default

transform: scale(0) with a matching transform-origin collapses it down to a single point at the chosen corner until hover.

3

overflow: hidden clips it

The parent clips anything outside its own bounds, so the oversized wedge never spills past the element's edges as it grows in on :hover.
