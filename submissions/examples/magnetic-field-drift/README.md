# Magnetic Field Drift

Magnetic Field Drift visualizes a magnetic source surrounded by animated field lines and moving particles.

## Usage
<div class="magnetic">
  <div class="field">
    <div class="line l1"></div>
    <div class="line l2"></div>
    <div class="magnet"></div>
  </div>
</div>

## Why is it useful?

It provides a visually rich way to represent invisible forces and works well in science, physics, education, and futuristic interfaces.

## Customization

Use --field to control field strength and data-direction to reverse particle animation.
