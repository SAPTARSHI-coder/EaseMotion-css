Why Cookie Banners Matter

Cookie consent banners are legally required in many regions (GDPR, CCPA) to inform users about data collection practices. This component provides a modern, accessible, and animated solution.

Features

  • ✅ Smooth slide-up entrance animation
  • ✅ Accept/Decline buttons with hover effects
  • ✅ "Learn More" link for privacy policy
  • ✅ Dismissible with smooth fade-out
  • ✅ Fully accessible with ARIA attributes
  • ✅ Respects prefers-reduced-motion
  • ✅ Mobile-responsive design

How It Works

The banner appears automatically when the page loads (or after a delay). Users can accept or decline cookies, and the banner smoothly animates out of view. The component uses EaseMotion's ease-slide-up for entrance and ease-fade-out for exit.
