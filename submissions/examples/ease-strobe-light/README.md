# ⚡ Strobe Light

## Strobe Effect On Interaction — Dynamic Light Show

### Description

**Strobe Light** is an electrifying, interactive light show that puts the power of a professional strobe at your fingertips. Click, tap, or start the strobe to experience a dynamic, pulsating light effect that syncs to your chosen beat — complete with flashing colors, pulsing objects, and mesmerising patterns that transform the screen into a dance floor.

The experience is intense and immersive. When activated, the screen pulses with bursts of light in your chosen color, while geometric objects — circles, squares, and triangles — dance and pulse in sync with the strobe. The effect is both visually striking and deeply engaging, creating an atmosphere of energy, rhythm, and excitement. Whether you're setting the mood for a party, creating a dramatic visual for a presentation, or simply enjoying the hypnotic pulse of light, Strobe Light delivers an exhilarating experience.

But this is more than just a simple flash effect — it's a fully customizable light show. Choose from six different **patterns** — Pulse, Double, Triple, Wave, Heartbeat, and Random — each creating a unique rhythm and feel. Control the **speed** (BPM) from a slow pulse to a rapid 300 BPM frenzy. Adjust the **brightness** to control the intensity of each flash. Pick your **color** from any hue imaginable. Start the strobe to run continuously, or trigger single flashes with a click or tap.

The visual experience is rich and layered. The strobe effect isn't just a flat flash — it's accompanied by radial bursts of light, pulsing geometric objects, and a dynamic background that comes alive with energy. The objects pulse and sway in sync with the beat, creating a cohesive, immersive experience that feels alive.

Whether you're looking to create an energetic atmosphere, a dramatic visual effect, or simply enjoy the hypnotic pulse of light and color, Strobe Light delivers with stunning visual quality, responsive controls, and endless creative possibilities.

### Features

- ⚡ **Interactive Strobe Effect** — Start/stop the strobe, trigger single flashes, or tap the canvas for instant feedback

- 🎯 **Six Dynamic Patterns** — Pulse, Double, Triple, Wave, Heartbeat, and Random — each with unique rhythm and feel

- ⚡ **Adjustable Speed (BPM)** — Control the pace from 20 to 300 BPM for slow pulses to rapid-fire strobes

- 💡 **Brightness Control** — Adjust flash intensity from subtle glow to blinding burst

- 🎨 **Customizable Color** — Pick any color for the strobe light effect

- 🔄 **Pulsing Objects** — Geometric shapes (circles, squares, triangles) that dance and pulse in sync

- 💥 **Single Flash Mode** — Trigger a dramatic single flash with the click of a button or tap on the canvas

- 📊 **Real-Time Status** — Track strobe status, BPM, and active pattern at a glance

- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Control** — Space to start/stop, F to flash, P to change pattern, arrow keys for BPM

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Support** — Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

---

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Start the strobe** with the ▶️ Start button, press Space, or tap the canvas

3. **Watch the light show** as the screen pulses with color and objects dance

4. **Adjust the speed (BPM)** slider to control the strobe rhythm (20-300 BPM)

5. **Adjust the brightness** slider to control flash intensity (10-100%)

6. **Pick a color** with the color picker to customize the strobe light

7. **Change patterns** with the 🔄 Pattern button or press P

8. **Trigger a single flash** with the 💥 Flash button, press F, or tap the canvas when stopped

9. **Stop the strobe** with the ⏹️ Stop button or press Space again
