## 🚀 Usage
1. Clone or copy the files into your project.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Hover over the badge to see the laser ray trace animation.

---

## 🛠️ Customization
- **Laser speed** → Adjust `animation: laser 2.5s` for faster/slower effect.
- **Glow colors** → Modify `box-shadow` and gradient stops for different neon palettes.
- **Badge size** → Adjust padding and font-size in `.laser-badge`.
- **Angle** → Change `rotate(25deg)` for different laser directions.

---

## 📖 Accessibility
- Large, clear text for readability.
- Smooth motion avoids harsh flicker.
