CSS Hamburger Variants

Pure CSS hamburger menu animations. Click the icons to see them transition without a single line of JavaScript.

1. Spin

Spins 180 degrees while transforming into an 'X'.

2. Collapse

The middle line fades out, top and bottom lines cross.

3. Arrow Left

Transforms into a directional back arrow.

4. Minus

Outer lines collapse into the center to form a minus.

5. Elastic

A bouncy, elastic transformation into an 'X'.
