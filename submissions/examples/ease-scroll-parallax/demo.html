Hardware Accelerated Depth

Notice how the background layers above moved at different speeds as you scrolled down. Historically, achieving this required attaching JavaScript listeners to the window's scroll event, performing calculations on every single pixel scrolled, and updating inline DOM styles—leading to severe jitter on mobile devices.

By utilizing the modern CSS animation-timeline: scroll() specification, we pass the mathematical depth calculations directly to the browser's hardware-accelerated compositor.
