Rounded Tag Badge

Small pill-shaped labels for cards, headers, and UI status tags.

This demo shows a simple CSS-only badge utility for labels like Featured, New, Draft, Beta, and Pro. It is lightweight, reusable, and easy to apply across many interfaces.
