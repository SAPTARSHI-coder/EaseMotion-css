Rotate-Fade Popover

A pure CSS popover that rotates and fades in on open, built for gaming-hub style layouts (player cards, match stats, quick info). Click a trigger button to open its popover, then click the trigger again or press Esc-equivalent by clicking elsewhere to close it. Enable "prefers reduced motion" in your OS settings to see the reduced-motion fallback.
