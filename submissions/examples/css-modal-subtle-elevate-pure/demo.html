Pure CSS Modal: Subtle Elevate

A minimalist, JavaScript-free modal utilizing the checkbox hack, featuring a highly refined, understated pop-in animation and soft drop shadows to simulate delicate physical elevation.

Quiet Design

This modal avoids aggressive physics. It utilizes a micro-interaction approach: starting at `scale(0.97)` and translating up just `12px` on the Y-axis. The drop shadow is wide, heavily blurred, and low opacity to simulate a piece of paper resting gently above the interface.
