Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the answers to the most common queries regarding our app.

How does FinFlow link to my bank?
FinFlow connects to your financial institutions using Plaid, a secure third-party provider trusted by thousands of FinTech apps. We never store your direct bank logins, credentials, or security question answers on our servers.
Is my money safe with FinFlow?
Yes, completely. All transaction data is encrypted end-to-end using 256-bit AES protection. Any money swept into savings vaults is stored in partner banks insured by the FDIC up to $250,000, ensuring your assets are protected.
How much does the average user save?
Our historical averages show that users using our automated sweeps, subscription monitors, and custom budgeting targets save an average of $430 in their first month.
