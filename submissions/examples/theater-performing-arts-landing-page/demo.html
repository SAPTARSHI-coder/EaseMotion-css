A Century of Grandeur

The Royal Grand Hall

Opened in 1902, the Royal Grand Hall is one of the world's most acoustically perfect and architecturally grand performing arts venues. Our historic auditorium features gilded gold leaf, plush velvet seating, and state-of-the-art stage technology that seamlessly supports both classical and modern staging.
