# Jellyfish Float · Glowing Jellyfish Pulse 🪼

A mesmerizing animated scene featuring glowing jellyfish with bioluminescent pulses, floating movements, and trailing tentacles. Create a calming, hypnotic underwater experience perfect for backgrounds, relaxation apps, or visual showcases.

## Features

- 🪼 **Glowing Jellyfish** – Bioluminescent jellyfish with pulsing bodies
- ✨ **Pulse Animation** – Gentle pulsing glow and body expansion
- 🌊 **Floating Movement** – Drifting and floating with natural motion
- 🎨 **Multiple Colors** – Random color variants (pink, blue, purple, green, gold, cyan)
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation with button or Space key
- 🔄 **Reset** – Regenerate jellyfish with new positions and colors
- 🌟 **Tentacle Trails** – Animated tentacles with wave motion
- 💫 **Bioluminescent Dots** – Glowing spots along the body
- 🌌 **Ocean Background** – Deep ocean gradient with light rays and bubbles

## How to Use

1. Watch the jellyfish float and pulse naturally
2. Press **⏸️ PAUSE** or **Space** to pause/resume animation
3. Adjust **Speed** slider to control animation speed
4. Press **RESET** or **R** to regenerate jellyfish
5. Each reset creates new colors and positions

## Controls

| Action | Method |
|--------|--------|
| Pause/Resume | Click button / Space |
| Reset | Click reset / R key |
| Speed | Drag slider (1-10) |

## Technical Details

- **Jellyfish:** 3 jellyfish with unique colors and movements
- **Body:** Elliptical dome with radial gradient and highlights
- **Pulse:** Sinusoidal scaling and glow intensity
- **Tentacles:** Segmented lines with wave motion (8-14 segments each)
- **Movement:** Drift with floating oscillation
- **Background:** Deep ocean gradient with light rays and particles
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Number of Jellyfish:** Change `numJellyfish` variable
- **Colors:** Modify `randomColor()` return values
- **Speed Range:** Adjust slider min/max attributes
- **Tentacle Count:** Change `tentacleCount` calculation
- **Movement:** Adjust drift, float, and pulse parameters
