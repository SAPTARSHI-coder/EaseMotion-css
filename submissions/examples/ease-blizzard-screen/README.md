# ❄️ Blizzard Screen

## Whiteout Blizzard Effect — Immersive Storm Simulation

### Description

**Blizzard Screen** is a stunning, immersive whiteout blizzard simulation that transforms your screen into a raging winter storm. Thousands of snow particles swirl, drift, and cascade across a dark, atmospheric landscape, creating a mesmerizing visual experience that captures the raw power and beauty of a severe blizzard.

The effect is nothing short of hypnotic. Snowflakes of varying sizes dance through the air, driven by powerful gusts of wind that sweep across the screen. The whiteout effect gradually obscures visibility, mimicking the disorienting experience of being in a real blizzard where the sky and ground blend into a single, featureless white void. Particles glow with subtle luminescence, leaving faint motion trails as they race across the canvas.

But this isn't just a passive visual — you control the storm. Adjust the **density** to go from a light flurry to a full-scale blizzard. Control the **wind speed** to see snow race horizontally or drift lazily. And the **whiteout slider** lets you dial in the perfect level of visibility, from clear winter scenes to complete whiteout conditions where you can barely see a few feet ahead.

Whether you're looking for a mesmerizing screensaver, a calming winter ambiance for your workspace, or a dramatic visual effect for creative projects, Blizzard Screen delivers with stunning visual fidelity, buttery-smooth performance, and intuitive controls that put the storm's power at your fingertips.

### Features

- ❄️ **Thousands of Particles** — Each snowflake is individually simulated with its own physics, size, opacity, and movement patterns

- 💨 **Dynamic Wind System** — Adjustable wind speed creates everything from gentle drifting to raging gusts

- 🌫️ **Whiteout Effect** — Control visibility from clear winter scenes to complete whiteout conditions

- 🎨 **Stunning Visuals** — Glowing particles, motion trails, atmospheric lighting, and realistic storm clouds

- 📊 **Real-Time Stats** — Track particle count and visibility levels at a glance

- 🎮 **Interactive Controls** — Adjust density, wind speed, and whiteout intensity in real-time

- ⌨️ **Keyboard Shortcuts** — Space to pause/resume, R to reset, ↑/+ to increase density, ↓/- to decrease density

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Optimized** — Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

- 🎯 **Responsive Design** — Beautiful on any screen size from phone to 4K monitor

### How to Experience the Blizzard

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server or internet required

2. **Watch the blizzard unfold** as thousands of snow particles swirl across the screen

3. **Adjust the density** with the slider (1 = light flurry, 10 = full blizzard)

4. **Control wind speed** to change how fast and horizontally snow moves (1 = calm, 10 = raging)

5. **Set the whiteout level** to control visibility (0% = clear, 100% = complete whiteout)

6. **Pause the action** with the ⏸️/▶️ button or press the Spacebar

7. **Reset the blizzard** with the 🔄 button or press R

8. **Increase/decrease density** with the ➕/➖ buttons or press ↑/↓ or +/- keys

### Technical Architecture

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Canvas Rendering** | Hardware-accelerated 2D canvas for smooth 60fps performance |
| **Particle System** | Each snowflake is an independent particle with physics and rendering |
| **Wind Physics** | Directional force with gusts and turbulence for natural movement |
| **Whiteout Engine** | Multi-layer overlay system with fog, noise, and visibility control |
| **Depth Sorting** | Particles rendered in depth order for realistic layering |
| **Performance Optimization** | Automatic particle limiting and efficient rendering techniques |

### Physics Parameters

| Parameter | Range | Description |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Density | 1-10 | Number of active snow particles (50-650) |
| Wind Speed | 1-10 | Horizontal force applied to particles |
| Whiteout | 0-100% | Visibility reduction and fog effect |
| Particle Size | 1-5px | Randomized per particle |
| Particle Speed | 0.5-3px/frame | Randomized per particle |
| Depth Layers | 0-1 | Background to foreground layering |
