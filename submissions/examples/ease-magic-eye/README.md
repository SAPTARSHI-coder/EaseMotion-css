# Magic Eye · Hidden 3D Image Emerges 👁️

An interactive magic eye stereogram where a hidden 3D image emerges from repeating patterns. Watch as the stereogram reveals itself with a mesmerizing animation!

## Features

- 👁️ **Magic Eye Stereogram** – Hidden 3D image emerges
- 🌀 **Reveal Animation** – Smooth transition from pattern to 3D
- 🎨 **Colorful Pattern** – Repeating colorful stereogram pattern
- 🎮 **Reveal/Hide Controls** – Show or hide the 3D image
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust reveal speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset to hidden state
- ⌨️ **Keyboard Shortcuts** – R to reveal, H to hide, Space to pause, X to reset

## How to Use

1. Click **REVEAL** or press **R** to watch the hidden 3D image emerge
2. Click **HIDE** or press **H** to hide the 3D image
3. Watch the pattern transition from random to stereogram
4. Press **Space** or click **PAUSE** to pause/resume animation
5. Adjust **Speed** slider to control reveal pace
6. Press **X** or click **RESET** to reset

## Technical Details

- **Stereogram:** Disparity-based 3D image in repeating pattern
- **Depth Map:** Multiple 3D shapes (sphere, ring, star)
- **Reveal Animation:** Smooth transition with easing
- **Pattern Generation:** Colorful repeating HSL pattern
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Depth Shapes:** Modify depthMap generation
- **Pattern:** Change pattern colors and frequency
- **Reveal Speed:** Adjust slider range (1-10)
- **Disparity:** Modify maxDisparity value
- **Pattern Size:** Adjust patternWidth and patternHeight
