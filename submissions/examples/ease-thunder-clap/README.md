# ⚡ Thunder Clap

## Thunder Rumbles With Visual Flash — Dramatic Storm Experience

### Description

**Thunder Clap** is a dramatic, immersive storm simulation that brings the raw power and awe-inspiring beauty of thunder and lightning to your screen. With each strike, brilliant forks of lightning tear across the sky, followed by deep, rumbling thunder that shakes the very air, creating a visceral, cinematic experience that captures the majesty of nature's most dramatic spectacle.

The experience is powerful, thrilling, and visually stunning. A dark, stormy sky fills the screen, heavy with brooding clouds and electric tension. When you strike, a brilliant flash of lightning illuminates the scene — jagged bolts of electricity fork across the sky, their bright white-hot cores surrounded by ethereal blue-white glows. The flash is blinding, painting the entire scene in brilliant light before fading into the darkness. Then comes the thunder — a deep, rumbling force that seems to shake the screen itself, with visual shockwaves radiating outward and particles of energy dancing in the aftermath.

The visual richness is extraordinary and cinematic. Lightning bolts are rendered with incredible detail — multiple jagged segments with bright cores, glowing edges, and intricate branching patterns that fork and split like real lightning. Each bolt is unique, with organic, unpredictable paths that feel authentic and alive. The flash illuminates the scene with a brilliant white light that fades gradually, while shockwaves ripple outward in concentric rings, and glowing particles drift through the air like embers of electrical energy. The storm clouds roll and churn in the background, adding depth and atmosphere.

But this is more than just a beautiful animation — it's an interactive experience that puts the power of the storm at your fingertips. **Strike** the thunder with a click, tap, or press of the Spacebar, and watch as lightning explodes across the sky and thunder rumbles through the scene. Adjust the **intensity** to control the power of each strike — from a gentle rumble to a window-rattling boom. Control the **rumble** duration to make the thunder roll on and on or fade quickly. Fine-tune the **flash duration** to make the lightning linger or flash in an instant. Enable **auto-thunder** for a mesmerizing, ever-changing storm that strikes on its own, creating a continuous dramatic performance.

Whether you're seeking a dramatic visual experience, a powerful interactive tool for storytelling, a mesmerizing screensaver, or simply a moment of awe-inspiring beauty, Thunder Clap delivers a stunning, responsive experience that captures the raw power and breathtaking beauty of thunder and lightning.

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Click the canvas** (or press Space) to strike thunder and watch lightning flash across the sky

3. **Watch the lightning** — jagged bolts fork across the sky with brilliant flashes

4. **Feel the rumble** — deep thunder rolls through the scene with visual shockwaves

5. **Adjust the intensity** slider for gentle rumbles or powerful booms (10-100%)

6. **Adjust the rumble** slider for long, rolling thunder or quick strikes (10-100%)

7. **Adjust the flash duration** slider for lingering light or instant flashes (10-100%)

8. **Enable auto-thunder** with the ▶️ Auto button for a continuous dramatic storm

9. **Reset** with the 🔄 Reset button to clear all effects
