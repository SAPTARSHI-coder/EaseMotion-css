# 💡 Fog Lamp

## Beam Cuts Through Fog — Atmospheric Volumetric Light

### Description

**Fog Lamp** is a stunning, atmospheric simulation that brings the dramatic effect of a powerful beam of light cutting through dense fog to life on your screen. Watch as a warm, golden beam pierces through swirling fog particles, creating a mesmerizing volumetric light effect that feels incredibly real and immersive.

The experience is hauntingly beautiful. A detailed fog lamp sits in a dark, misty environment, its beam reaching out into the unknown. As the light travels through the fog, it illuminates tiny particles suspended in the air, creating a visible beam that scatters and softens with distance. The fog itself is alive — drifting, swirling particles of mist that dance in the darkness, occasionally catching the light and glowing briefly before fading back into shadow.

But this is more than just a visual — it's a fully interactive experience. **Drag the lamp** to move it around the scene and watch the beam sweep across the fog. **Scroll** or use the beam angle slider to adjust the width of the light from a tight spotlight to a wide flood. Control the **fog density** from a light haze to a thick, impenetrable mist. Adjust the **intensity** to make the beam blaze brightly or glow softly. Toggle the lamp on and off, or turn the fog off entirely to see the beam in clear air.

The visual fidelity is exceptional. The beam is rendered using volumetric techniques — multiple layers of light that fade with distance, scatter through the fog, and create realistic god rays. The fog particles are individually simulated, each drifting independently and interacting with the light. The result is a rich, atmospheric scene that feels like standing in a misty landscape with a powerful light.

Whether you're a designer studying lighting effects, a developer learning about volumetric rendering, or simply someone who appreciates the beauty of light in mist, Fog Lamp delivers a captivating, interactive experience that showcases the magic of light and fog.

### Features

- 💡 **Realistic Volumetric Beam** — Multi-layered light beam with scattering, attenuation, and god rays

- 🌫️ **Dynamic Fog System** — Hundreds of individually simulated fog particles that drift and swirl

- 🎮 **Fully Interactive** — Drag the lamp, adjust beam angle, fog density, and intensity in real-time

- 🔦 **Adjustable Beam Angle** — Control from 20° (tight spotlight) to 180° (wide flood)

- 💡 **Intensity Control** — Adjust beam brightness from subtle glow to blazing light

- 🌫️ **Fog Density Control** — From clear air to thick, impenetrable fog

- 📊 **Real-Time Status** — Track lamp state, fog density, and beam angle at a glance

- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Control** — Space to toggle lamp, R to reset, F to toggle fog, arrow keys for beam angle

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Support** — Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile

- 🎨 **Rich Visual Effects** — God rays, light scattering, fog particles, and atmospheric haze

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Watch the beam** as it cuts through the fog with realistic volumetric lighting

3. **Drag the lamp** (the light fixture) to move it around the scene

4. **Adjust the beam angle** with the slider or scroll on the canvas (20-180°)

5. **Adjust the fog density** slider from clear to thick fog (0-100%)

6. **Adjust the intensity** slider for brighter or dimmer beam (10-100%)

7. **Toggle the lamp** on/off with the 💡 Toggle button or press Space

8. **Toggle the fog** on/off with the 🌫️ Fog button or press F

9. **Reset the lamp position** with the 🔄 Reset button or press R
