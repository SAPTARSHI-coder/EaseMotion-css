# Newton Cradle · Balls Click Back and Forth ⚡

An interactive Newton's cradle simulation with realistic physics, swinging balls, and satisfying click sounds. Watch the energy transfer between balls!

## Features

- ⚡ **Realistic Physics** – Pendulum dynamics with energy transfer
- 🔴 **Swing Animation** – Click to start the motion
- 🔊 **Click Sounds** – Satisfying ball collision sounds
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Ball Count** – Choose 3, 5, or 7 balls
- 📊 **Energy Indicator** – Visual energy display
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset to rest position
- 🎨 **Color-coded Balls** – Each ball has unique hue

## How to Use

1. Click **SWING** or press **Space** to start the motion
2. Watch the balls swing and transfer energy
3. Listen to the satisfying click sounds
4. Adjust **Speed** for faster or slower motion
5. Adjust **Ball Count** for 3, 5, or 7 balls
6. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
7. Press **Space** or click **PAUSE** to pause/resume animation
