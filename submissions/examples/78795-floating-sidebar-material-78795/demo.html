IMPLEMENTATION

Material depth

The sidebar uses a restrained shadow and rounded surface to establish elevation without blocking page content. On narrow screens it becomes a horizontal navigation strip.

Elevation

Layered shadows separate the navigation from the document surface.

Hierarchy

Spacing and typography keep navigation secondary to the main content.

Adaptation

The layout changes orientation rather than forcing a desktop sidebar onto small screens.
