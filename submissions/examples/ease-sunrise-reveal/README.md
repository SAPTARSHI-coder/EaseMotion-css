# Sunrise Reveal · Sun Rises on Interaction 🌅

An interactive sunrise reveal with sun rising on interaction, atmospheric colors, and glowing effects. Watch the dawn break!

## Features

- 🌅 **Sunrise Reveal** – Sun rises on interaction
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust rise speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Glow Control** – Adjust glow intensity (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset to night
- ✨ **Ray Effects** – Glowing sun rays
- 🐦 **Birds** – Flying birds appear

## How to Use

1. Click **RISE** or press **Space** to watch the sun rise
2. Click again or press **Space** to reset to night
3. Adjust **Speed** for rise pace
4. Adjust **Glow** for brightness
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
6. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation

## Controls

| Action | Method |

- **Sun Animation:** Smooth rise with interpolation
- **Sky Colors:** Night to dawn transition
- **Rays:** Animated light rays
