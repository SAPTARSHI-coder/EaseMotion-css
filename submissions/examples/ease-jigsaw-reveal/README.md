# Jigsaw Reveal · Pieces Assemble into Image 🧩

An interactive jigsaw puzzle where pieces assemble to reveal a beautiful image with satisfying animation. Watch as scattered pieces come together!

## Features

- 🧩 **Jigsaw Puzzle** – Pieces with realistic jigsaw edges
- 🎨 **Colorful Image** – Beautiful abstract image to reveal
- ✨ **Smooth Assembly** – Pieces glide into place with easing
- 💫 **Scatter Effect** – Pieces fly apart on command
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust assembly speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset puzzle
- ⌨️ **Keyboard Shortcuts** – A to assemble, S to scatter, Space to pause, R to reset

## How to Use

1. Click **ASSEMBLE** or press **A** to watch pieces come together
2. Click **SCATTER** or press **S** to scatter pieces
3. Watch pieces glide into place with smooth easing
4. Press **Space** or click **PAUSE** to pause/resume animation
5. Adjust **Speed** slider to control assembly pace
6. Press **R** or click **RESET** to reset the puzzle

## Technical Details

- **Jigsaw Pieces:** 30 pieces (6×5 grid) with jigsaw edges
- **Assembly:** Smooth easing interpolation (cubic ease-out)
- **Image:** Generated abstract colorful image
- **Edges:** Tabs and notches on all four sides
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Grid Size:** Modify `COLS` and `ROWS` (6×5)
- **Piece Colors:** Adjust image generation colors
- **Assembly Speed:** Modify slider range (1-10)
- **Scatter Radius:** Adjust radius in scatter function
- **Easing:** Modify ease function in update method
