# Animated Music Player Submission

## 1. What does this do?
It provides a beautiful, interactive music/audio player with animated visualizer, rotating album art, full playback controls, volume control, and an interactive playlist. Perfect for music streaming apps, podcast platforms, and audio content websites.

## 2. How is it used?
Wrap your audio player in the `.music-player` container. The component includes album art with rotation animation, audio visualizer bars, progress bar with drag-to-seek, playback controls (play/pause, next/prev, shuffle, repeat), volume control, and a clickable playlist.

## 3. Why is it useful?
- Essential component for music streaming and podcast platforms
- Animated audio visualizer with bouncing bars
- Rotating album art animation when playing
- Full playback controls with shuffle and repeat
- Interactive progress bar with drag-to-seek
- Volume control with slider
- Clickable playlist with track selection
- Smooth animations and transitions throughout
- Fully accessible with ARIA labels
- Includes `prefers-reduced-motion` support for accessibility
- Mobile-responsive design
- Easy to customize colors and styles
- Maintainer can easily standardize this as `.ease-music-player-[YOUR_INITIALS]` in the core library.
