Animated Star Rating (Glassmorphism Edition)

Note: Built with 100% pure CSS (Zero JS). Uses flex-direction: row-reverse combined with the general sibling combinator (~) to create a fully functioning rating system. The Glassmorphism aesthetic is achieved through backdrop-filter: blur, semi-transparent backgrounds, and thin highlighted borders. Active stars utilize filter: drop-shadow for a glowing effect against the dark glass. Fully keyboard accessible and respects prefers-reduced-motion.
