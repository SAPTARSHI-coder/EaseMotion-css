Liquid Blob Button

A tactile call-to-action whose layered shapes stretch, merge, and morph like a small liquid blob on hover.

Hover interaction

Fluid call to action

Move the pointer over a button to see the surface distort while the label remains stable and readable.

Component anatomy

Layered liquid motion

01

Core surface

The primary shape anchors the button and carries the label.

02

Secondary blob

A blurred layer expands into the core to create a merging impression.

03

Organic radius

Border-radius values animate between asymmetric shapes.

04

Depth

Soft shadows and highlights provide a fluid material quality.

05

Focus

Keyboard focus remains visible even when the blob is moving.

06

Reduced motion

Motion is removed for users who request reduced animation.

Usage

Designed for real interfaces

  • Keep button labels short and action-oriented.
  • Use one strong accent color per action.
  • Preserve contrast between text and animated surface.
  • Pair the effect with a visible focus state.
