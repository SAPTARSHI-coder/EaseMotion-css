CSS-Only Off-Canvas Sidebar Navigation

Click the hamburger icon to open the sidebar. Notice how it works completely without JavaScript!

The state is managed using the "Checkbox Hack" combined with CSS sibling selectors and transitions.

You can close the menu by clicking the 'X' button inside the sidebar, clicking the backdrop, or clicking the hamburger icon again.
