# Rainbow Fish · Shimmering Rainbow Colors 🌈

A mesmerizing animation featuring rainbow fish with shimmering iridescent colors, dynamic rainbow gradients, and graceful underwater movements. Watch as fish swim in elegant patterns while their colors shift through the entire rainbow spectrum.

## Features

- 🌈 **Rainbow Colors** – Fish shimmer through the full color spectrum
- 🐟 **Graceful Movement** – Elegant figure-eight swimming patterns
- ✨ **Iridescent Shimmer** – Scales shimmer with dynamic rainbow effects
- 🎨 **Dynamic Gradients** – Body colors flow through rainbow hues
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation with button or Space key
- 🔄 **Reset** – Regenerate fish with new positions and colors
- 🌊 **Underwater Environment** – Deep ocean with light rays and bubbles

## How to Use

1. Watch rainbow fish swim gracefully through the water
2. See their colors shimmer and shift through the rainbow
3. Press **PAUSE** or **Space** to pause/resume animation
4. Adjust **Speed** slider to control animation pace
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to regenerate with new positions

## Technical Details

- **Rainbow Colors:** HSL with time-based hue shifting
- **Fish Movement:** Figure-eight patterns with smooth interpolation
- **Shimmer Effect:** Sinusoidal color and scale animation
- **Fish Anatomy:** Body, tail (split), dorsal fin, pectoral fin, eye
- **Scales:** 8 individual scales with color and size animation
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Number of Fish:** Change `numFish` variable (currently 6)
- **Fish Size:** Modify `this.size = 20 + Math.random() * 25`
- **Movement Pattern:** Adjust `patternRadius` and `patternSpeed`
- **Rainbow Speed:** Change `time * 20` multiplier in hue calculation
- **Shimmer Speed:** Modify `shimmerSpeed` values
