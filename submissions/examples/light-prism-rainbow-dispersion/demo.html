🔬 How Light Prism Works

🌞

White Light Entry

A beam of pure white light enters the prism from the left, ready to be dispersed.

💎

Refraction

The glass prism refracts light at different angles based on wavelength, splitting it into colors.

🌈

Dispersion

Each color bends differently, creating a beautiful rainbow spectrum of 12 distinct rays.

✨

Visualization

Watch as the prism rotates and the light spreads, creating a stunning optical display.
