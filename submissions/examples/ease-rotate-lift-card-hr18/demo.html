How the lift works

1

One combined transform

translateY(-8px) rotate(-3deg) runs on the same transform property, so the lift and the tilt animate together instead of as two separate motions.

2

Shadow deepens in step

box-shadow is transitioned alongside transform, growing both its blur and offset on hover to sell the sense of the card lifting off the page.

3

Direction is a single variable

The modifier only flips the sign on the rotation angle — everything else about the transition stays identical between the two directions.
