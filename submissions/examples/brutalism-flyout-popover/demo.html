BRUTAL UI

RAW & UNAPOLOGETIC

Note: Built with 100% pure CSS (Zero JS). Utilizes the Checkbox Hack pattern to manage the popover state globally.

This variant embodies the Neo-Brutalism aesthetic:

  • High Contrast: Bright colors against stark white and pure black.
  • Hard Shadows: No blur or softness. Shadows are solid black offsets (6px 6px 0 0 #000).
  • Thick Borders: Solid 3px black borders outline every interactive element.
  • Physical Interactions: Hovering or clicking elements physically translates them down to "press" into their shadow.
