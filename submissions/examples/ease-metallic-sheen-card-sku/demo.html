Design & Optimization Highlights

Reflective Metallic Sweep

On hover or keyboard focus, a realistic reflection gradient sweep sweeps across the card face. This creates a tactile, physical feel to interactive elements.

Compositor Performance

The sweep relies entirely on 3D transforms (`translateX` and `skewX`) which run on the GPU compositor thread rather than repaint-heavy layout changes, keeping scrolling at 60 FPS.

Accessibility First

Text color contrast ratios are explicitly calculated and adjusted per metallic background to exceed WCAG 2.1 standards. Keyboard tabs trigger the sweep via `:focus-within` and `:focus-visible`.
