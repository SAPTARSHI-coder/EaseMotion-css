ease-modal

A modal built on the native dialog element.

A centered modal dialog on <dialog> with a blurred/dimmed ::backdrop and a scale + fade entrance. Closes via backdrop click, Escape, or the close button — all native <dialog> behavior.

Triggers

Tip: press Esc or click the dimmed backdrop to close.
