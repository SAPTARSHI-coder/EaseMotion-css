
---

## 🚀 Usage
1. Clone or copy the files into your project.
2. Open `demo.html` in your browser.
3. The product card will display with a diagonal "New" tag label.

---

## 🛠️ Customization
- **Tag text** → Change inner text of `.product-tag` (e.g., "Hot", "Limited").
- **Colors** → Adjust `background` and `color` in `.product-tag`.
- **Angle** → Modify `transform: rotate(-45deg)` for different diagonal styles.

---

## 📖 Accessibility
- Product image uses `alt` text for screen readers.
- Tag is decorative, so `pointer-events: none` ensures focus remains on product content.
