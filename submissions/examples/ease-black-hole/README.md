# 🌀 Black Hole – Pulls Light and Elements into Event Horizon

> An immersive black hole component where particles, light, and matter are pulled into the event horizon with gravitational lensing, accretion disk, and photon ring effects.

---

## 📖 Description

The **Black Hole** component creates an immersive cosmic experience where particles, light, and matter are pulled into the event horizon with realistic gravitational effects. Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, it features mass and spin controls, particle physics, photon rings, and accretion disk effects for a mesmerizing celestial experience.

### 🎯 Core Features

- **Gravitational pull** – particles spiral into the event horizon
- **Mass control** – adjust mass from 0-100% affecting gravity strength
- **Spin control** – adjust spin from 0-100% affecting accretion disk
- **Particle system** – colorful particles with trails and glow effects
- **Photon rings** – light rings burst when particles are absorbed
- **Accretion disk** – rotating disk with gravitational lensing
- **Particle counter** – tracks number of active particles
- **Interactive controls** – buttons, click on display, keyboard shortcuts
- **Responsive design** – adapts to different screen sizes

### ⌨️ Interaction Options

| Method | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Click** | Click on display to feed the black hole |
| **Buttons** | Feed, Reset |
| **Keyboard** | `Space` or `F` – Feed · `R` – Reset |
