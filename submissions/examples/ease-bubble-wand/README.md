# Bubble Wand · Bubbles Float from Wand 🫧

An interactive bubble wand that blows colorful floating bubbles with realistic physics and iridescent effects. Watch bubbles float, shimmer, and pop!

## Features

- 🫧 **Realistic Bubbles** – Iridescent floating bubbles
- 💨 **Blow Control** – Click to blow bubbles
- 🎚️ **Bubble Size** – Adjust bubble size (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Wind Control** – Adjust wind effect (1-10)
- ✨ **Iridescent Effect** – Rainbow shimmering colors
- 📊 **Bubble Counter** – Tracks bubbles
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🗑️ **Clear** – Clear all bubbles
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset everything

## How to Use

1. Click **BLOW** or press **Space** to start blowing bubbles
2. Click again to stop
3. Watch bubbles float and shimmer
4. Adjust **Bubble Size** for larger/smaller bubbles
5. Adjust **Wind** for drift direction
6. Click **CLEAR** or press **C** to clear all bubbles
7. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
