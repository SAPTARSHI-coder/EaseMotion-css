# Frisbee Glide · Glides Through Air 🥏

An interactive frisbee with realistic gliding physics, smooth flight path, and catching mechanics. Throw the frisbee and watch it glide through the air!

## Features

- 🥏 **Realistic Glide** – Physics-based flight with lift
- 🎚️ **Glide Control** – Adjust glide effect (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Angle Control** – Adjust launch angle (1-10)
- ✨ **Flight Trail** – Glowing trail effect
- 📊 **Speed Display** – Real-time velocity readout
- 🔄 **Throw Counter** – Tracks throws
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset frisbee
- 🎯 **Catch Animation** – Returns to hand

## How to Use

1. Click **THROW** or press **Space** to throw the frisbee
2. Watch it glide through the air
3. Adjust **Glide** for more/less lift
4. Adjust **Angle** for launch trajectory
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
6. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation

## Technical Details

- **Physics:** Gravity with lift and glide
- **Rendering:** Frisbee with tilt and spin
- **Controls:** Real-time parameter adjustment
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Glide Range:** Modify slider range (1-10)
- **Angle Range:** Modify slider range (1-10)
- **Frisbee Size:** Adjust `size` (35)
- **Trail Length:** Modify `maxTrail` (25)
- **Colors:** Adjust frisbee gradient colors
