# 🌧️ Rain Stick

## Rain-like Sound On Tilt — Interactive Rain Simulation

### Description

**Rain Stick** is a mesmerizing, interactive simulation that brings the soothing sound and visual beauty of falling rain to your screen. Like a traditional rain stick, tilting the instrument creates a cascade of gentle rain-like sounds and falling droplets, transforming your device into a virtual rain maker that responds to your every movement.

The experience is calming, immersive, and deeply satisfying. A beautifully crafted wooden rain stick sits at the center of a dark, atmospheric space, its surface adorned with intricate wood grain, decorative bands, and small holes where the rain emerges. When you click and drag to tilt the stick, or give it a shake, hundreds of glowing droplets cascade down like a gentle rainstorm. Each drop falls with its own unique trajectory, leaving soft trails behind as it descends, while tiny sparkles of light dance in the air like raindrops catching the light.

The visual richness is extraordinary and soothing. The rain stick is rendered with warm, earthy wood tones — rich browns, amber, and golden hues with realistic wood grain patterns, decorative bands, and end caps. When tilted, the stick comes alive with an inner glow as rain begins to fall. Hundreds of individual raindrops cascade from the stick, each one a delicate teardrop shape with soft blue-white glow, leaving shimmering trails as they fall. Sparkle particles burst forth like tiny diamonds, adding a magical, ethereal quality to the rain. The drops fall with organic, natural motion — some fast, some slow, some straight, some drifting — creating a rich, layered visual experience that feels alive.

But this is more than just a beautiful animation — it's an interactive instrument that responds to your touch. Click and drag on the stick to tilt it, and watch the rain flow with gravity — tilt left and the rain falls to the left, tilt right and it cascades to the right. The more you tilt, the heavier the rain becomes. Give it a **shake** for a sudden downpour, or enable **auto-rain** for a mesmerizing continuous performance. Adjust the **rain density** to control how many drops fall — from a light, gentle drizzle to a heavy, soothing downpour. Control the **tilt sensitivity** to make the stick respond more or less dramatically to your movements. Change the **particle size** for fine, misty drops or larger, more dramatic raindrops.

Whether you're seeking a calming, meditative experience to relax your mind, a beautiful interactive art piece for contemplation, a soothing soundscape for sleep or focus, or simply a moment of peaceful beauty, Rain Stick delivers a stunning, responsive experience that captures the gentle, soothing magic of falling rain.

### Features

- 🌧️ **Realistic Rain Simulation** — Hundreds of individual raindrops with natural physics, trails, and organic motion

- 🌊 **Interactive Tilt Control** — Click and drag to tilt the stick and control the direction and intensity of rain

- 💧 **Adjustable Rain Density** — Control how many drops fall from light drizzle to heavy downpour (10-100%)

- 🌊 **Tilt Sensitivity** — Adjust how dramatically the stick responds to your movements (10-100%)

- ✨ **Particle Size Control** — Fine-tune drop size from misty to dramatic (1-10)

- 🌊 **Shake Effect** — Give the stick a shake for a sudden, satisfying downpour

- ▶️ **Auto-Rain Mode** — Sit back and watch the rain fall in a mesmerizing, ever-changing pattern

- 💫 **Sparkle Particles** — Tiny shimmering sparkles dance in the air like raindrops catching light

- 📊 **Real-Time Status** — Track rain state, drop count, and tilt angle

- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Control** — Space to shake, A for auto, R for reset, arrow keys for tilt and density

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Support** — Click, drag, or touch to tilt and control the rain on any device

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Click and drag on the rain stick** to tilt it and watch the rain flow

3. **Tilt left** to make the rain fall to the left, **tilt right** to make it fall to the right

4. **Shake the stick** with the 🌊 Shake button or press Space for a sudden downpour

5. **Adjust the rain density** slider for light drizzle to heavy rain (10-100%)

6. **Adjust the tilt sensitivity** slider for more or less responsive movement (10-100%)

7. **Adjust the particle size** slider for fine mist or large drops (1-10)

8. **Enable auto-rain** with the ▶️ Auto button for a continuous mesmerizing performance

9. **Reset** with the 🔄 Reset button to clear all rain and return to stillness
