# 🔔 Cymbal Crash

## Cymbal Ripples On Impact — Dynamic Sound Visualization

### Description

**Cymbal Crash** is a stunning, dynamic visualization that brings the explosive energy of a cymbal crash to life on your screen. With each impact, beautiful golden ripples radiate outward from the cymbal's surface, accompanied by a shower of brilliant sparks, creating a mesmerizing visual symphony of light and motion.

The experience is visceral and captivating. A beautifully rendered brass cymbal sits centered in a dark, atmospheric space, its surface gleaming with metallic highlights and subtle lathe lines. When you click the cymbal, press Space, or trigger an auto-crash, the cymbal erupts with energy — it vibrates intensely, shimmering with golden light, while concentric ripples of warm, golden energy pulse outward across the screen. Each ripple expands with organic fluidity, shimmering and sparkling as it travels, creating a cascading wave of light that feels alive. Brilliant sparks burst from the impact point, arcing across the darkness like fireworks, each one trailing a glowing path before fading into the void.

The visual richness is extraordinary. The cymbal itself is meticulously detailed — a warm, golden gradient with subtle rings, a glowing center bell, and a wavy edge that trembles with each impact. The ripples are not just simple circles — they're complex, shimmering rings with inner glows, sparkle points, and a warm, golden color palette that shifts and pulses with organic motion. The sparks explode outward in dynamic patterns, each one carrying its own hue of warm gold, amber, and brass, leaving glowing trails as they dance across the canvas.

But this is more than just a beautiful animation — it's an interactive experience you control. Adjust the **impact force** to control the intensity of the crash — from a gentle tap to a thunderous strike. Control the **ripple speed** to make the waves of energy flow fast and energetic or slow and graceful. Change the **ripple count** to create a single, powerful wave or a cascade of multiple ripples. Trigger a crash with a click, set it to auto-crash for a mesmerizing light show, or reset the scene to stillness.

Whether you're seeking a dynamic visual experience, a satisfying interactive toy, a tool for rhythm visualization, or simply a moment of explosive beauty, Cymbal Crash delivers a stunning, responsive experience that captures the energy and elegance of a cymbal's sound.

### Features

- 🔔 **Realistic Cymbal Rendering** — Detailed brass cymbal with gradient shading, lathe rings, center bell, and wavy edge

- 🌊 **Dynamic Ripple System** — Beautiful golden ripples that radiate outward on impact with shimmer and sparkle

- 💥 **Impact Simulation** — Cymbal vibrates, wobbles, and glows with each crash

- ✨ **Spark Particle System** — Brilliant sparks burst from the impact point with glowing trails

- 🎚️ **Adjustable Impact Force** — Control crash intensity from gentle tap to powerful strike (10-100%)

- 🌊 **Ripple Speed Control** — Adjust wave propagation speed from slow and graceful to fast and energetic (10-100%)

- ✨ **Ripple Count Control** — Choose from 1-12 ripples per crash for varied visual effects

- ▶️ **Auto-Crash Mode** — Sit back and watch the cymbal crash automatically in a mesmerizing loop

- 📊 **Real-Time Status** — Track cymbal state, ripple count, and impact force

- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Control** — Space to crash, A for auto, R for reset, arrow keys for force

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Support** — Click or tap the cymbal to crash on any device

---

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Click the cymbal** (or press Space) to crash it and watch the ripples and sparks explode outward

3. **Adjust the impact force** slider for harder or softer crashes (10-100%)

4. **Adjust the ripple speed** slider for faster or slower wave propagation (10-100%)

5. **Adjust the ripple count** slider for single or multiple ripples (1-12)

6. **Enable auto-crash** with the ▶️ Auto button to watch the cymbal crash repeatedly

7. **Reset** with the 🔄 Reset button to clear all ripples and sparks

8. **Experiment** with different force, speed, and count combinations for unique visual effects
