Structure

1

Container handles the row

.stats-container is a flex-wrap row that centers and gaps its children — it doesn't care how many cards it holds.

2

Card is self-contained

.stat-card carries its own padding, radius, and shadow, so it drops into any layout — grid, flex row, or sidebar — without depending on its parent.

3

Optional pieces bolt on

.stat-icon and .stat-trend are both optional — omit either and the card still lays out correctly with just a number and a label.
