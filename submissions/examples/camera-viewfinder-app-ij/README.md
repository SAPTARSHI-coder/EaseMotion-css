# ease-camera-viewfinder-app

A camera viewfinder where the corner marks pulse, the crosshair scans, and the REC dot blinks.

## Run
Open `demo.html` directly in a browser to watch the viewfinder frame.

## Notes
- The four corner marks pulse around the frame.
- The crosshair line scans up and down the screen.
- The REC dot blinks in the top bar.
