Smooth Accordion

Native <details> that animates open and closed using grid-template-rows, so the panel transitions to its real height without a hard-coded max-height.

Why not max-height?

A max-height transition needs a guessed ceiling. Too low clips the content; too high makes the easing feel wrong, because the visible part of the transition is only a fraction of the declared range.

What does grid do instead?

A single-row grid can transition grid-template-rows from 0fr to 1fr. The browser resolves 1fr to the content's real height, so the easing curve maps onto the true distance.

Is it accessible?

It is a native disclosure widget. Keyboard behaviour, the expanded state and in-page find all come from the browser rather than from ARIA you have to maintain.
