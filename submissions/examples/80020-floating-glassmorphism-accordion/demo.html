GLASSMORPHISM / ACCORDION

Floating Questions

A clean floating accordion with translucent glass surfaces, soft gradients, and smooth interactions.

01 What is glassmorphism?

Glassmorphism is a visual design style that combines translucent surfaces, background blur, subtle borders, and soft shadows to create a frosted-glass appearance.

02 Why use a floating accordion?

Floating cards visually separate each section from the background while keeping related information compact and easy to scan.

03 Does it require JavaScript?

No. The accordion uses the native HTML details and summary elements, making it lightweight and dependency-free.

04 Is it responsive?

Yes. The layout adapts to smaller screens with flexible spacing, typography, and card dimensions.
