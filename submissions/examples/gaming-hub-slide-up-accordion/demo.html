Nexus Global Championship

The Nexus Global Championship utilizes a double-elimination bracket format for the qualifiers, transitioning into a single-elimination Gauntlet for the top 16 teams. All matches leading up to the grand finals will be Best-of-3 (BO3).

  • Open Qualifiers: May 15th - May 20th
  • Group Stage: May 25th - June 2nd
  • The Gauntlet: June 10th - June 14th

The total base prize pool is set at $5,000,000 USD, which increases through community compendium purchases. Distribution is weighted heavily towards the top 8 performing teams.

The reigning champions receive 45% of the total pool, alongside exclusive in-game Mythic tier cosmetics permanently memorialized in the Hall of Fame.

All participants must run the Vanguard hypervisor-level anti-cheat client during official matches. Network latency is artificially equalized to 25ms across all regions to ensure fair competitive integrity.

Rosters are strictly locked 72 hours prior to the Open Qualifiers. Emergency substitutions are only permitted under verified medical circumstances.
