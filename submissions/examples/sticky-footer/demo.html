Why Sticky Footers?

Sticky footers are useful for keeping important actions visible at all times. They are commonly used for sign‑up prompts, purchase buttons, or navigation shortcuts.

With CSS, you can achieve this effect using positioning and flexbox layouts. The footer here includes a CTA button encouraging users to subscribe.
