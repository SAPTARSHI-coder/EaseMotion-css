Safari Border-Radius Bleed Fix

Resolving a notorious WebKit rendering bug where scaled child images bleed outside the rounded corners of an overflow: hidden parent container.

The WebKit Bleed Bug

Hover the image in Safari (iOS/macOS). The sharp square corners of the scaling image will suddenly pierce through the rounded corners of the card!

Buggy Card

The Hardware Mask Fix

Applying transform: translateZ(0) (or a webkit radial mask) forces Safari to process the border-radius clipping on the same hardware layer as the scaling image, stopping the bleed.
