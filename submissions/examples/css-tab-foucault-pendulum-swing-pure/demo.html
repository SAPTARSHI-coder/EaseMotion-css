The Principle of Inertia

An object at rest stays at rest and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by an unbalanced force.

Velocity Constant

Conservation of Momentum

Momentum is defined as the product of a system's mass multiplied by its velocity. In a closed system, the total momentum remains constant.

Equation p = m × v

Gravitational Oscillation

A pendulum relies on gravity as a restoring force. As it swings away from equilibrium, gravity pulls it back, creating a continuous periodic motion.
