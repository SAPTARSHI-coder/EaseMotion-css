Force Animation Utility

A strategic override for prefers-reduced-motion to ensure absolutely essential visual cues (like loading spinners) still animate even when motion is disabled.

Standard Spinner

Will STOP spinning if the user enables "Reduce Motion" in their OS settings.

Forced Spinner

Uses .force-animation. Will CONTINUE spinning even if the user enables "Reduce Motion".

How to test this locally:

If you are on Windows: Go to Settings > Accessibility > Visual Effects > Turn off "Animation effects".

If you are on Mac: Go to System Settings > Accessibility > Display > Turn on "Reduce motion".

Once activated, the first spinner will freeze, but the second one will keep spinning!
