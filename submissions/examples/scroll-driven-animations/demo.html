Native Scroll-Driven Animations

A pure CSS implementation of reading progress bars and scroll-linked reveal effects, natively powered by the browser's scrolling engine.

Scroll Down

Keep scrolling to see the images reveal themselves. Notice the blue progress bar at the very top of the screen growing as you read.

Hardware Accelerated

Because these animations are bound to the scroll timeline natively in CSS, they run on the compositor thread. This means zero JavaScript execution cost.

No Intersection Observers

Before modern CSS, revealing elements on scroll required complex Intersection Observer JavaScript. Now, we just use animation-timeline: view().

Perfect Sync

The animation progress is mathematically locked to your scroll wheel. If you scroll backwards, the animation perfectly reverses in real-time.
