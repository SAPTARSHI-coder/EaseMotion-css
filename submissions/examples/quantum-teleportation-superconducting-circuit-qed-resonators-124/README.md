\# Superconducting Circuit QED Resonators Component



\## Technical Overview

The Superconducting Circuit QED Resonators component is an advanced visualization module built for the EaseMotion CSS framework, simulating circuit quantum electrodynamics (cQED), coplanar waveguide (CPW) microwave resonators, strong matter-light coupling, and dispersive qubit readout architectures.

\## Circuit QED Simulation Design

1\. \*\*cQED Flux Dynamics\*\*: The `.cqed-flux` elements utilize multi-directional counter-rotating conic gradients to mimic microwave photon circulation and transmission line tracks.

2\. \*\*cQED Boundary Ring\*\*: The `.cqed-boundary-ring` models coplanar waveguide cavity boundary tracking using dashed structural tracks and rotational scaling.

3\. \*\*cQED Core\*\*: The `.cqed-core` visualizes central artificial atom-photon interaction and dispersive readout coupling nodes through rapid scale pulsations and intense blue illumination.
