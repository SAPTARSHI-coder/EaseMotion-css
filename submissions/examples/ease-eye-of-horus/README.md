# Eye of Horus · Eye Opens with Mysterious Glow 👁️

An interactive Eye of Horus that opens with a mysterious glow, featuring ancient Egyptian mystical atmosphere, smooth opening animation, and radiant light effects.

## Features

- 👁️ **Eye of Horus** – Ancient Egyptian symbol
- 🔮 **Mysterious Glow** – Radiant light effects
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust opening speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Glow Control** – Adjust glow intensity (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset to closed state
- ✨ **Mystical Effects** – Glow, iris, and pupil details

## How to Use

1. Click **OPEN** or press **Space** to open the eye
2. Click **CLOSE** or press **Space** again to close
3. Adjust **Speed** for opening pace
4. Adjust **Glow** for light intensity
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset
6. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation

## Controls

| Action | Method |
|--------|--------|
| Open/Close | Open/Close button / Space |
