# 🌙 Moonbeam

## Moonbeam Follows Cursor — Ethereal Light Experience

### Description

**Moonbeam** is a breathtaking, ethereal experience where a luminous moonbeam follows your cursor like a magical spotlight from the moon. As you move your mouse or drag your finger across the screen, a beautiful, glowing beam of moonlight tracks your every movement, creating a mesmerizing dance of light and shadow that feels like you're guiding a piece of the moon itself.

The experience is pure enchantment. A soft, silvery beam of light radiates from your cursor position, casting a gentle glow across a dark, starlit night landscape. The beam is not just a simple circle — it's a rich, layered cone of light with a bright core, soft edges, and ethereal particles that drift and swirl within it like fireflies caught in moonbeams. The light is alive, pulsing gently with its own rhythm, responding to your every movement with smooth, organic fluidity.

The visual richness is extraordinary. The night sky is filled with twinkling stars and a beautifully rendered crescent moon with subtle craters and soft glow. The moonbeam itself has incredible depth — a bright, white-hot core, a soft blue-silver glow, and outer wisps of light that fade into the darkness. Particles of light dance within the beam, moving like dust motes in a sunbeam but with the ethereal quality of moonlight. The beam changes color through different moon modes — from soft silver to warm gold to mystical purple — each creating a unique atmospheric mood.

But this is more than just a beautiful visual — it's an interactive experience you control. Switch between **seven moon modes** — Soft (silver-blue), Lunar (bright white), Silver (cool silver), Mystic (purple-blue), Warm (golden), Aurora (teal-blue), and Crystal (ice-blue) — each with its own emotional resonance. Adjust the **intensity** to make the beam blaze brightly or glow softly. Control the **beam width** from a tight spotlight to a wide, sweeping glow. Fine-tune the **glow** amount for subtle atmospheric lighting or dramatic luminescence. Toggle the beam on and off, or reset its position with a click.

Whether you're seeking a calming, meditative experience, a beautiful interactive art piece, a tool for focus and mindfulness, or simply a moment of ethereal beauty, Moonbeam delivers a captivating, responsive experience that feels like guiding moonlight itself.

### Features

- 🌙 **Cursor-Following Moonbeam** — A luminous beam of light that tracks your every movement with smooth, organic fluidity

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Move your cursor** (or drag your finger on touch devices) to guide the moonbeam

3. **Watch the beam follow** with smooth, organic fluidity and ethereal particles

4. **Cycle through modes** with the 🌀 Mode button or press M

5. **Adjust the intensity** slider for brightness control (10-100%)

6. **Adjust the beam width** slider for spotlight to flood (20-200)

7. **Adjust the glow** slider for atmospheric glow intensity (0-100%)

8. **Toggle the beam** on/off with the ✨ Toggle button or press Space

9. **Reset the position** with the 🔄 Reset button or press R
