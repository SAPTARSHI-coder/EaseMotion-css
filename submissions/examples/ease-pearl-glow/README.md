# Pearl Glow · Glowing Pearl with Iridescence 🪷

A mesmerizing glowing pearl with iridescent shimmer, rainbow reflections, and soft luminescent glow. Watch as the pearl floats gently, pulsing with luminous light while iridescent colors shift across its surface like a true natural pearl.

## Features

- 🪷 **Glowing Pearl** – Soft luminescent pearl with radiant glow
- 🌈 **Iridescent Shimmer** – Rainbow colors shift across the surface
- ✨ **Pulsing Light** – Gentle pulse animation with glow intensity
- 🌊 **Floating Motion** – Pearl floats gracefully in the water
- 🎨 **Iridescent Rings** – Concentric rings with shifting colors
- 💎 **Highlights & Specks** – Realistic reflections and iridescent specks
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation with button or Space key
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset pearl animation

## How to Use

1. Watch the glowing pearl float and pulse with iridescent light
2. See rainbow colors shimmer across the pearl's surface
3. Press **PAUSE** or **Space** to pause/resume animation
4. Adjust **Speed** slider to control animation pace
5. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset

## Technical Details

- **Pearl Rendering:** Radial gradients with HSL color shifting
- **Iridescence:** Time-based hue rotation (360° cycle)
- **Animation:** Float, pulse, and iridescent shift with sinusoidal timing
- **Effects:** Glow gradients, highlights, specks, and rings
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Pearl Size:** Modify `this.radius = 80` in Pearl class
- **Float Speed:** Adjust `floatSpeed` (currently 0.01)
- **Pulse Speed:** Modify `pulseSpeed` (currently 0.015)
- **Iridescent Speed:** Change `iridescentSpeed` (currently 0.008)
- **Colors:** Modify HSL values in gradients
- **Specks Count:** Change `for (let i = 0; i < 12; i++)` loop
