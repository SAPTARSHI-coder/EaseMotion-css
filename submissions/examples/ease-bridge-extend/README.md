# Bridge Extend · Drawbridge Extends and Retracts 🌉

An interactive drawbridge that extends and retracts with realistic mechanics, chains, and castle details. Watch the bridge lower and raise!

## Features

- 🌉 **Drawbridge** – Realistic bridge with chains
- ⬇️ **Extend** – Lower the bridge
- ⬆️ **Retract** – Raise the bridge
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust movement speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Length Control** – Adjust bridge length (1-10)
- 📊 **Angle Display** – Real-time angle readout
- 🔄 **Extension Counter** – Tracks extensions
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset to retracted position

## How to Use

1. Click **EXTEND** or press **E** to lower the drawbridge
2. Click **RETRACT** or press **R** to raise the drawbridge
3. Adjust **Speed** for movement speed
4. Adjust **Length** for bridge length
5. Press **RESET** or **X** to reset
6. Press **PAUSE** or **P** to pause/resume animation
