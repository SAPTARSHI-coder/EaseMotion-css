Step Progress Bar

Reusable progress steps for forms, onboarding, and checkout flows.

This demo showcases a clean CSS-only step progress bar with completed, active, and upcoming states. It is useful for onboarding experiences, setup wizards, and multi-step forms where users need a clear sense of progress.
