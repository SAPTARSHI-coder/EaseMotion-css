3D Flip Z-Index Fix

Solving the infamous stacking context bug where transform: rotateY() forcibly flattens absolute child elements, trapping their z-index inside the parent plane.

The Buggy Flip

Hover the card. The "Sale!" badge has z-index: 999, but it gets flattened into the card and clips into the background because the 3D transform creates a trapped stacking context.

The Fixed Flip

By applying transform-style: preserve-3d to the parent and translating the badge along the Z-axis, it beautifully breaks out of the flat plane and orbits above the card.
