motion-sensitivity-pulse

Pulse badges that respect motion sensitivity.

A demonstration of an accessible pulse animation that honors prefers-reduced-motion automatically and exposes a manual motion-sensitivity toggle (a data-motion attribute on the page root) so users can calm the animation on demand — fixing the issue where .ease-badge-pulse ran forever regardless of accessibility settings.
