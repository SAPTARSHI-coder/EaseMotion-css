# Peacock Display · Feathers Fan Out Beautifully 🦚

A stunning interactive peacock display featuring beautifully fanning feathers with iridescent colors, eye patterns, and graceful animation. Click to watch the peacock fan its magnificent tail feathers!

## Features

- 🦚 **Beautiful Display** – Feathers fan out with stunning iridescent colors
- 🎨 **Iridescent Shimmer** – Feathers shift through beautiful color gradients
- 👁️ **Eye Patterns** – Detailed feather tips with eye patterns and glow
- 🪶 **24 Feathers** – Each feather with unique color and size
- 🎮 **Speed Control** – Adjust animation speed (1-10)
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation with button
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset peacock to starting position
- 🌿 **Garden Environment** – Lush green background with foliage

## How to Use

1. **Click** on the canvas or press **Space** to fan the feathers
2. Click again to close the fan
3. Watch the iridescent colors shimmer across each feather
4. See the detailed eye patterns on each feather tip
5. Press **PAUSE** to pause/resume animation
6. Adjust **Speed** slider to control animation pace
7. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset

## Controls

| Action | Method |
|--------|--------|
| Toggle Fan | Click canvas / Space |
| Pause/Resume | Click button |

## Technical Details

- **Feather System:** 24 feathers with individual color offsets and phases
- **Fan Animation:** Smooth interpolation with speed control
- **Iridescence:** HSL color shifting with time-based animation
- **Eye Patterns:** Multi-ring feather tips with glow effects
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Feather Count:** Modify `this.featherCount = 24`
- **Feather Length:** Adjust `80 + Math.random() * 40`
- **Fan Speed:** Modify `this.fanSpeed = 0.04`
- **Colors:** Adjust HSL values in feather gradients
- **Eye Size:** Change `8 + Math.random() * 6`
