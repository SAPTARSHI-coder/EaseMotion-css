## 🚀 Usage
1. Clone or copy the files into your project.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Hover over the badge to see particles burst outward.

---

## 🛠️ Customization
- **Particle count** → Add/remove `<span class="particle">` elements.
- **Colors** → Adjust particle background color for different palettes.
- **Burst distance** → Modify `transform: translate(...)` in `@keyframes`.
- **Speed** → Change `animation: burst 0.8s` for faster/slower bursts.

---

## 📖 Accessibility
- Clear text inside badge for readability.
- Motion is smooth and short to avoid distraction.
