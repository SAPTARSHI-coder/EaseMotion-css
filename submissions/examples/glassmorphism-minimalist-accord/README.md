
---

## 🚀 Usage
1. Clone or copy the files into your project.
2. Open `demo.html` in your browser.
3. Click or focus on headers to expand/collapse sections.

---

## 🛠️ Customization
- **Colors** → Adjust background gradient and text colors.
- **Blur strength** → Modify `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` for stronger/weaker glass effect.
- **Animation speed** → Change `transition: max-height 0.4s` for faster/slower expansion.

---

## 📖 Accessibility
- Uses `:focus-within` for keyboard navigation.
- Headers are buttons for proper semantics.
- Content expands on focus for screen reader compatibility.
