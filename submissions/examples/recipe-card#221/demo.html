Spicy Garlic Noodles

By Kitchen Studio

⭐ 4.7

A quick and flavorful noodle recipe with garlic, chili, and a simple savory sauce. Perfect for a fast weeknight dinner.

Prep 10 min
Cook 15 min
Serves 2

Ingredients

Noodles Garlic Chili Soy Sauce Spring Onion

Quick Steps

  1. Boil the noodles until tender, then drain.
  2. Sauté garlic and chili until fragrant.
  3. Add noodles, sauce, and spring onion. Toss and serve hot.
