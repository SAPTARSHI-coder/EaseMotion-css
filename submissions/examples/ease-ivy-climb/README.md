# 🌿 Ivy Climb – Ivy Climbs Upward on Click

> An interactive ivy climbing component where ivy grows upward on click with 4 styles (Classic, Vines, Thick, Autumn), growth control (0-100%), and organic leaf growth.

---

## 📖 Description

The **Ivy Climb** component creates an interactive ivy climbing experience where ivy grows upward on click with organic leaf growth. Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, it features 4 styles (Classic, Vines, Thick, Autumn), growth control (0-100%), and organic leaf growth for a satisfying nature experience.

### 🎯 Core Features

- **Ivy climbing** – ivy grows upward on click with organic motion
- **4 styles** – Classic, Vines, Thick, Autumn with unique colors
- **Growth control** – adjust growth from 0-100%
- **Organic leaves** – leaves grow along the vines
- **Vine system** – main vine with branching side vines
- **Interactive controls** – slider, buttons, keyboard shortcuts
- **Responsive design** – adapts to different screen sizes

### 🍃 Ivy Styles

| Style | Icon | Colors | Description |
|-------|------|--------|-------------|
| **Classic** | 🌿 | Green | Classic ivy |
| **Vines** | 🌱 | Dark Green | Vine-focused growth |
| **Thick** | 🌳 | Deep Green | Thick, lush ivy |
| **Autumn** | 🍂 | Orange | Autumn colors |

### ⌨️ Interaction Options

| Method | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Slider** | Adjust Growth (0-100%) |
| **Buttons** | Climb (toggle), Reset |
| **Keyboard** | `Space` or `C` – Climb · `R` – Reset |

### ✨ Ivy Experience

When climbing:
1. **Ivy grows** – vines extend upward
2. **Growth applies** – controls ivy height
3. **Leaves appear** – organic leaf growth
4. **Style applies** – changes colors and leaf size
5. **Status updates** – shows current state

### 💡 Why Use This Component?

- Perfect for nature scenes, meditation apps, or calming interfaces
- Satisfying ivy growth with organic motion
- Self-contained – no external dependencies or frameworks
- Easy to customize (styles, colors, growth behavior)
- 4 styles for variety
- Growth control for customization

### 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – semantic structure with Canvas
- **CSS3** – gradients, backdrop-filter, transforms
- **JavaScript (ES6)** – Canvas API, requestAnimationFrame, organic growth

### 🎨 Design Philosophy

This component embodies **EaseMotion CSS's animation-first philosophy** through:

- **Organic motion** – ivy grows naturally
- **Leaf growth** – leaves appear gradually
- **Visual feedback** – status updates, growth display, controls
- **Calming atmosphere** – nature-inspired visuals
- **Human-readable classes** – `.ivy-controls`, `.style-btn`, `.ivy-text`

### 🔧 Customization Options

| Property | Location | Default |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Styles | `styles` object | 4 styles |
| Growth range | `growthSlider` | 0-100% |
| Leaf count | `maxLeaves` | 20-100 |
| Vine points | `numPoints` | 10-40 |
