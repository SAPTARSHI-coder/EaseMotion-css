Ripple-Wave Navbar

Hover or tab to any link — a ripple pulses out from its center, while a continuous neon wave drifts slowly along the bar's bottom edge underneath everything, giving the whole navbar a sense of motion even at rest.

Custom properties

PropertyDefaultControls
--ease-ripple-duration-hr18900msHow long each link's hover ripple takes to fully expand.
--ease-wave-duration-hr185sHow long one full cycle of the ambient wave drift takes.
