
---

## 🚀 Usage
1. Clone or copy the files into your project.
2. Open `demo.html` in your browser.
3. The traffic light cycles through red, yellow, and green automatically.

---

## 🛠️ Customization
- **Cycle duration** → Adjust `animation: ... 6s` for faster/slower transitions.
- **Colors** → Change background colors of `.red`, `.yellow`, `.green`.
- **Size** → Modify `.light` width/height for larger or smaller lights.

---

## 📖 Accessibility
- Uses `role="img"` and `aria-label="Traffic light animation"` for screen readers.
- Keyboard focus not required since it’s a decorative animation.
