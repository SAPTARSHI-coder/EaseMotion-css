
---

## 🚀 Usage
1. Clone or copy the files into your project.
2. Open `demo.html` in your browser.
3. Hover or focus on the question to expand the answer.

---

## 🛠️ Customization
- **Animation speed** → Adjust `transition: max-height 0.5s` for faster/slower expansion.
- **Answer size** → Modify `max-height` in `.faq-answer`.
- **Colors** → Change background and text colors in `.faq-item` and `.faq-question`.

---

## 📖 Accessibility
- Uses `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` for screen readers.
- Focus styles ensure keyboard navigation works.
